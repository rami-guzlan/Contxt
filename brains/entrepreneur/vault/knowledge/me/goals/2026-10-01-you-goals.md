# What this Brain is for

Run my two businesses without dropping things, and keep my investing separate from both.

## What do you work on, and for whom?

Loopline, the company I run now; Brewbox, which I sold; and a handful of angel checks.

## Where do things slip today? What do you forget, redo, or explain over and over?

Promises I make on calls, investor updates, and which numbers I last told which investor.

## What should every AI always know about how you like work done?

The decision I need to make first, with a recommendation, then options with their cost, then details.

## What should an AI never do for you without asking?

Email an investor, commit to a date, or sign anything.

## When it reads your meetings and files, what should it leave out?

Small talk, anything said off the record, and other founders' numbers.

## What is in motion right now?

Loopline seed round
Hire a first account executive
Plumbing launch

## A month from now, how will you know it is working?

Investor updates take an hour, not a day, and nothing I promise on a call gets lost.
