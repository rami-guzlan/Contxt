Brewbox was Riley's coffee subscription from 2018 to 2021. It peaked at 4,100 subscribers and shut down when shipping costs erased the margin. Lessons: prove unit economics before spending on growth, and never depend on a single supplier. The full postmortem is in references/brewbox-postmortem.md.
