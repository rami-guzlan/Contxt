# Loopline pitch notes

## The one-line story

Small field service crews run their week on whiteboards and group texts. Loopline puts the
schedule, the dispatch and the customer updates in one place a technician can use from a
truck.

## Proof points

- 212 paying companies, 9% month over month MRR growth (September 2026).
- Payback on customer acquisition in 7 months.
- Technicians open the app 11 times a day on average.

## Objections we hear

- "We already use a big field service suite": built for 200 technicians, not 12.
- "My team will not adopt it": onboarding takes one afternoon, done by our team.
