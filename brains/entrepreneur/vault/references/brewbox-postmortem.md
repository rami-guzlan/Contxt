# Brewbox postmortem (2021)

## What happened

Brewbox sold monthly coffee subscriptions from small roasters. Growth came from paid social
and peaked at 4,100 subscribers. In 2021 shipping costs rose 40% in eight months and the
largest roaster raised prices. Margin per box went from $6.20 to $0.80.

## What we got wrong

1. We spent on growth before the unit economics were proven at scale.
2. One roaster supplied 70% of the coffee.
3. We priced annual plans too low to absorb cost increases.

## What we would do again

- Talking to 20 customers every month.
- Writing a monthly investor update, even with bad news.
