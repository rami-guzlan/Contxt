# What this Brain is for

Run our household without dropping things: meals, Ollie's school and soccer, money, and the trips we keep planning.

## What do you work on, and for whom?

Our family of three in Denver: me, Jamie and Ollie, who is 7. I am a UX researcher working from home; Jamie works shifts.

## Where do things slip today? What do you forget, redo, or explain over and over?

Who picks Ollie up on Jamie's shift days, which bills are due, and what is in the fridge before I plan dinners.

## What should every AI always know about how you like work done?

Short lists, plans as bullet points, prices with tax included, the simpler option first.

## What should an AI never do for you without asking?

Book, buy or pay for anything, or share anything about Ollie.

## When it reads your meetings and files, what should it leave out?

Gossip from the school group chat, and anything medical unless I say so.

## What is in motion right now?

Half marathon in May
Japan trip in April
Kitchen refresh

## A month from now, how will you know it is working?

No missed pickups, and planning the week takes ten minutes on Sunday.
