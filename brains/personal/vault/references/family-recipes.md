# Family recipes

## Black bean tacos (Tuesday)

Black beans with cumin and lime, corn tortillas, cabbage slaw, salsa and avocado on the side.
20 minutes.

## Lentil soup (Monday)

Red lentils, carrots, onion, cumin and lemon. Make a double batch and freeze half. 40 minutes.

## Friday pizza

Store-bought dough, tomato sauce, mozzarella, and one topping each. Check every topping
label for peanuts. 30 minutes.
