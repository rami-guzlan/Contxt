# Taylor's Life (demo Brain)

A demo Brain for a made-up person. Taylor Brooks, Jamie, Ollie and every detail here are
invented for this demo.

Taylor keeps family life in one Brain so that every AI app knows the same things: the
peanut allergy, who does pickup when, the budget, the house's maintenance, and where each
plan stands. Health, money and family details are marked private.

What to try with it:

- Ask "plan dinners for next week" and see the allergy, the budget and the family's
  favorites arrive.
- Ask "where are we on the Japan trip?" for the latest progress and what to book next.
- Ask "can I book a dentist appointment on Wednesday at 3 PM?" and see the pickup clash.
