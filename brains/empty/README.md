# My Brain

This is your Brain: everything your AI apps should know about you and your work, as plain
Markdown files, with every change kept in git.

It starts empty. Fill it the easy way:

- In the Contxt app, first-time setup asks who you are, how you like work done and what you
  are working on, and files each answer here.
- In any connected AI app, just work. When you state a rule, a preference or a decision, the
  AI proposes it for your Brain, and you approve it in Review.
- Add files (proposals, briefs, plans) in the app or ask your AI to file one into a project.

Where things go:

- `vault/knowledge/<area>/<topic>/`: notes, one durable idea each.
- `vault/knowledge/projects/<project>/`: a project's brief and the notes that belong to it.
- `vault/skills/`: procedures your AI can follow (and the app can run).
- `vault/references/`: longer documents your AI can quote from.
- `vault/journal/`: each project's progress and the record of every decision.
- `vault/brain.json`: the areas, scopes and approval rules of this Brain.
