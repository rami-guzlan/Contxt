# /linkedin-post

Draft one LinkedIn post in Brightmill's voice from an idea, a podcast episode or a research
finding.

1. Find the one idea. If the input has several, pick the strongest and say which you left out.
2. Open with a first line under 12 words: a problem from the plant floor or a number from the
   research library.
3. Write 80 to 150 words. Short paragraphs. One idea.
4. End with a question a plant manager would answer from experience.
5. Put any link in a note for the first comment, never in the post.
6. Check the house style: numerals, Oxford comma, no exclamation marks, no em or en dashes.
7. Hand it back as a draft for Morgan's approval, with the source of every number listed below.
