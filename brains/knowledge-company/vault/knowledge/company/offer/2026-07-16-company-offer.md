Brightmill sells four things, from free to premium, and these are the prices.

- The Line Brief: a free weekly research newsletter, every Tuesday.
- Floor Notes: a podcast every other Thursday with a plant leader as guest.
- Brightmill Courses: self-paced courses of two to four hours, $290 per seat, with team plans from 10 seats at $240 per seat.
- Brightmill Circles: peer groups of eight plant leaders who meet monthly for six months, $4,800 per member.
