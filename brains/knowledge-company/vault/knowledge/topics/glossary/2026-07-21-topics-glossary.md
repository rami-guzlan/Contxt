OEE: overall equipment effectiveness, availability times performance times quality. SMED: single minute exchange of dies, cutting changeover time. 5S: sort, set in order, shine, standardize, sustain. Gemba: the place where the work happens. Kaizen: small continuous improvements made by the people doing the work.
