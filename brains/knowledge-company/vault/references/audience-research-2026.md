# What we learned from the 2026 survey

## The headline

Plant leaders are short on people, not on ideas. Most improvement work stalls because the
supervisors who should run it were promoted without being trained.

## What this means for marketing

1. Lead with the supervisor problem: it is the most shared pain in the survey.
2. Use the survey numbers from the research library, always with the source.
3. Show the floor: stories from named plants beat abstract frameworks.

## Segments

- Plant managers at 200 to 500 employees: hands-on, read on mobile before the shift.
- VPs of operations at 500 to 2,000 employees: plan budgets in the fall, want proof.
- CI leads: want templates and tools they can use the same week.
