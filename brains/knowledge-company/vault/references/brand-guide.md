# Brightmill brand guide

## Who we sound like

A plant manager who has seen a few things, talking to a peer over coffee before the shift
starts. Specific, calm, a little dry. We respect the reader's time and experience.

## Words we use

- Plant floor, line, shift, changeover, downtime, scrap, operators, supervisors.
- Numbers with units: minutes of downtime, percent of scrap, dollars per hour.

## Words we avoid

Revolutionary, game-changing, unlock, supercharge, cutting-edge, synergy, best-in-class.

## Headlines

Sentence case, under ten words, one promise. Good: "Cut changeover time without new
equipment". Weak: "Unlock the Future of Manufacturing Excellence".

## Visuals

Mill Blue #1F4E79 as the base, Safety Orange #F28C28 for one accent, Concrete #E7E6E3 for
backgrounds. Inter for all type. Real plants, real people, real equipment.
