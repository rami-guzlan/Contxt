# Brightmill Marketing (demo Brain)

A demo Brain for a made-up company. Brightmill, its people, its numbers and its research
are invented for this demo; no real company's data is in it.

Brightmill is a knowledge company for operations leaders at mid-size US manufacturers: a
weekly research newsletter, a podcast, self-paced courses and peer groups. Its marketing is
run by one person, Jordan Avery, and the CEO, Morgan Hale, approves everything external.

What to try with it:

- Ask your AI to "write a LinkedIn post about getting new supervisors ready" and see the
  voice rules, the audience and the approval rule arrive with the request.
- Ask "where are we on the webinar series?" and get the project's latest progress.
- Run the webinar-one-pager skill in the app to see a skill make a file.

The Brain is plain Markdown: `vault/knowledge` holds the notes, `vault/skills` the
procedures, `vault/references` the longer documents, and `vault/journal` each project's
progress.
