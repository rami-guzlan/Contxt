# What this Brain is for

Make every coding assistant follow how I build, so I stop repeating our conventions in every session.

## What do you work on, and for whom?

Backend services in Python and TypeScript, plus tidyq, an open-source queue library I maintain.

## Where do things slip today? What do you forget, redo, or explain over and over?

Our test and release conventions, which branch a change goes to, and why we made an architecture call six months ago.

## What should every AI always know about how you like work done?

Read the code before writing, small changes, a plan in three bullets before touching more than one file, never guess an API.

## What should an AI never do for you without asking?

Push, delete a branch, change a database, or add a dependency without asking.

## When it reads your meetings and files, what should it leave out?

Stack traces, one-off debugging, and anything from work repositories I am not allowed to share.

## What is in motion right now?

tidyq 1.0 release
Route planner migration
Learn Rust

## A month from now, how will you know it is working?

New sessions follow our conventions without a reminder, and reviews find fewer style fixes.
