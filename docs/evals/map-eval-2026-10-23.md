# Brain map evaluation, October 23, 2026

Release 1 plan, section 10.5. Does the Brain map help a connected AI answer, without over-pulling?

**Setup** (scripts/map_eval.py):
- **Model:** Claude Haiku 5.5 through `claude -p`, with only the Brain's MCP tools, connectors off, and at most 4 turns.
- **Questions:** 60, labelled before any run (scripts/map_eval_questions.json):
  - the 28 project requests of scope.json;
  - 12 gap questions, whose answer is in the Brain but missed by today's packet;
  - the first 20 short-answer questions of the frozen LongMemEval development cohort.
- **Scoring:** no model judge. An answer is correct when it holds an expected term and cites the needed note.
- **Not read:** the sealed Brains and the release cohort.
- **Run:** at main 5a1574a, once. An earlier attempt stopped after 13 questions on a usage limit, and its rows are discarded. The script now stops instead of scoring empty replies.

**The three conditions:**
1. **packet:** the packet only.
2. **map:** the packet plus the Brain map, with no pulls.
3. **map_pulls:** the packet plus the map, with the read tools available.

## Against the ship criteria

| Criterion | Result | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Correct answers rise on the gap questions | 5 to 6 of 12 with pulls; unchanged with the map alone | Barely: one question |
| No more than 1 point lower elsewhere | scope 27 to 25 (map) and 24 (pulls): 7 to 11 points lower | **Fails** |
| Unused pulls at most 20% | 50% scope, 25% gaps, 83% LongMemEval | **Fails** |
| Median tokens per answer up at most 25% | +43% scope, +50% to +56% gaps, +47% to +103% LongMemEval | **Fails** |

**Verdict:** the map does not pass its gate as built. Keep it behind the plan's rule (a missed gate keeps a feature limited) until a revision passes a fresh run.

## What the rows show

- **The map makes Haiku answer worse on questions the packet already answers.** On three scope questions it was right with the packet alone and wrong with the map: it followed the map's names instead of the packet's content.
- **Most gains come from pulls, and most pulls are wasted.**
  - On LongMemEval, 12 pulls added 2 correct answers, and 10 of the 12 pulled notes were never cited.
  - On the gap set, pulls closed 2 gaps out of 7 open.
- **The map's cost is about 155 to 300 tokens per packet before any pull.** That is more than the plan's 250-token cap suggests once the map's framing lines are counted.
- **Sample sizes are small:** 28, 12 and 20 questions, each run once. A one-question difference is within noise. The direction is consistent across all three sets, though.

## Next

Worth trying, each with its own fresh run (none tuned on these questions):
1. Send the map only when the packet's "nothing here" bar was close, rather than with every packet.
2. A stricter walkthrough rule for pulls: pull only a name the request itself mentions.
3. Count the map's framing inside its 250-token cap.
