# /wrap-up

Close this conversation so nothing that matters is lost and nothing that does not is kept.

1. Open the note `you-goals` to know what the Brain is for. Keep what serves it.
2. Look back over the whole conversation and list:
   - **Decisions** the person made, with the reason when they gave one.
   - **Rules, corrections and preferences** they stated that will apply beyond today.
   - **Facts** that changed or that the Brain did not have.
   - **Progress** on each project the conversation moved: what happened, where it stands now,
     what is next, and anything blocking it.
   - **Files** they shared or worked on that they will want again.
3. Leave out one-off instructions, drafts that went nowhere, and anything only true today.
4. Check each item against the Brain first. Skip what a note already says. When it changes a
   note, propose the new version and name the note it replaces.
5. Keep them:
   - decisions, rules, preferences and facts: propose each as a note, quoting the person in the
     evidence;
   - progress: record one update per project, with only the parts that changed;
   - files: add each to the project it belongs to.
6. End with a short receipt: what you kept, what applied at once, what waits for the person's
   yes, and what you left out on purpose.
