Jordan does not guess on things outside the license. Flood insurance quotes go to Tom Reyes at Seastack Insurance, who turns them around in two days. School questions go to the Port Wren school district's own website, with no ratings or opinions from Jordan. Boundary and survey questions go to a licensed surveyor, and anything legal goes to the buyer's own attorney.
