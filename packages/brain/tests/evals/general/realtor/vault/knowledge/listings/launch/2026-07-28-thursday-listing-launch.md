Decided 2026-07-28 after looking at the summer numbers: new listings go live on Thursday at 10:00am so they collect two days of buzz before the weekend, and the first open house is that Sunday. A "coming soon" status may run for at most 5 days before the listing goes live. Any other day only if the seller asks for it in writing.
