Jordan's standard listing agreement runs six months at a 5% total fee. Of that, 2.5% is offered to the buyer's agent if the seller agrees, and the seller can change the split. Wrenhaven Realty adds a $395 transaction fee paid at closing. Jordan goes down to 4.5% for a seller who is also buying through Jordan, and never lower without sign-off from the managing broker, Ellen Ruiz.
