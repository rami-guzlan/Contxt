Lena Ostrow of Lowtide Studio shoots every one of Jordan's listings. The standard package is 30 edited photos for $325; drone adds $150 and a twilight set adds $200, which Jordan only orders for water-view homes. Lena needs five days' notice in summer and delivers within 48 hours. Jordan pays Lena directly and never bills the seller for it.
