1. Open houses run Sunday 1:00 to 3:00pm; Jordan arrives at 12:15 and has the six directional signs out by 12:30. 2. Turn on every light, open the blinds, and set out 25 printed property sheets plus the seller's condition statement. 3. Each visitor signs in on the tablet with name, email and whether they already have an agent. 4. Signs come down by 3:30. 5. By Monday noon, draft a thank-you to each visitor who has no agent, and by Monday evening send the seller the head count and what people said.
