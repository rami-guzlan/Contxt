About 40% of Port Wren homes sit in flood zone AE or VE. Every listing, in a flood zone or not, states the zone from the town's flood map, whether the seller carries flood insurance and the current yearly premium, and whether an elevation certificate exists. If the seller has no certificate, Kim asks the town clerk for a copy before launch.
