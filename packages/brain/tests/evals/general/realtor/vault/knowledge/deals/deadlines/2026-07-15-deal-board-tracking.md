1. Within one business day of acceptance, Kim adds the property to the Deal Board spreadsheet with each date: inspection, appraisal, financing, walk-through and closing. 2. Kim sets calendar reminders for Jordan 3 days and 1 day before each one. 3. When a date is met or waived, Kim marks it done and files the signed release or amendment. 4. Jordan goes through the whole board every Monday at 8:00am and calls the other agent about anything at risk.
