Port Wren's sign bylaw allows temporary directional signs only from Friday 5:00pm to Sunday 6:00pm, and no more than six per open house. None may go on the harbor roundabout or the village green. Code enforcement fines $50 per sign and has been strict since July. Lindell Cove allows no off-site signs at all.
