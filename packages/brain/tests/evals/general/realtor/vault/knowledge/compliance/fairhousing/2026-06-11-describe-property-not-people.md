This covers MLS remarks, buyer emails, posts, flyers and anything said on a tour. Write about the house, the lot and measured distances, never about the kind of person it suits or the people around it. Do not suggest a buyer look in or avoid a neighborhood because of who lives there, and do not call any area safe or unsafe. If a client asks for that kind of guidance, say Jordan can't answer it and point them to public data instead.
