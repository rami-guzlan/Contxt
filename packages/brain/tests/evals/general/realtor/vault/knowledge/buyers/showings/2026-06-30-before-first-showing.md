No private tour until the buyer has signed Jordan's buyer agreement (90 days, either side can end it with 5 days' written notice) and sent a pre-approval letter or proof of funds. Open houses are exempt. If a buyer pushes back, Jordan offers a short call to walk through the agreement instead of skipping it.
