Jordan's go-to loan officer is Dana Okafor at Kettle Point Lending, cell 555-0187 (a text gets the fastest answer). Pre-approval letters come back within 24 hours, same day if the file is complete by noon. Dana will reissue a letter showing only the offer price so the seller never sees the buyer's full budget; ask Dana for it, not the buyer. Brokerage policy is to name two other lenders alongside Dana so buyers can compare.
