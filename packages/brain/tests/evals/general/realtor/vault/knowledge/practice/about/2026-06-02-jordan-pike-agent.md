Jordan Pike has been licensed since 2014 and hangs the license with Wrenhaven Realty, a nine-agent office on Front Street in Port Wren. Jordan works both sides (selling homes for owners and representing buyers) and closed 23 sales in 2025 at a median price of about $585,000. The territory is Port Wren plus the two villages next door, Lindell Cove and North Shelby. Kim Alvarado is Jordan's assistant and the only other person on the team.
