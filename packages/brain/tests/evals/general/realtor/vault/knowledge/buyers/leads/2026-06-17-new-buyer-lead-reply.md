Answer a new buyer within one hour between 8am and 7pm, or by 9am the next morning otherwise. Attach the "Buying in Port Wren" PDF guide. Ask four things: when they want to move, whether they have a pre-approval or are paying cash, their three must-haves, and which of the three towns they like. Offer a 20-minute phone call with two time options, and have Kim add them to the lead sheet.
