1. Pull closed sales from the last 6 months within half a mile (1 mile in North Shelby), in the same flood zone and within 20% of the home's square footage. 2. Drop estate sales and family transfers, then adjust about $40,000 for deeded water access and $18,000 per garage bay. 3. Give the seller three numbers: stretch, likely, and a 30-day sale. 4. Set the asking price just under a search break ($599,000 rather than $605,000). 5. Kim saves the comp sheet to the listing folder.
