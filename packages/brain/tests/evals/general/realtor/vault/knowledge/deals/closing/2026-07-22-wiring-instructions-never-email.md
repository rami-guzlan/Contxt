Fake "updated instructions" emails can empty a buyer's down payment in minutes, and it is the biggest money risk in any sale. Jordan and Kim never send, forward or confirm wiring details in writing. Every buyer email in the two weeks before closing carries a line telling them to phone Ninepin at the number on their engagement letter, never a number from an email. If a buyer mentions new or changed instructions, stop and call Renee Watts right away.
