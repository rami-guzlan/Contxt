New listings in Port Wren peak in April and May, and the strongest prices usually close in June and July. Second-home shoppers mostly look between Memorial Day and Labor Day, then disappear. November to February is quiet, with fewer homes for sale, but whoever turns up is serious. Jordan tells owners who can wait to list in late March.
