Unless the buyer wants something different, Jordan writes offers with earnest money of 1% of the price (minimum $5,000), a 10-day inspection contingency, 21 days for appraisal, 30 days for financing and closing in 45 days. The deposit goes to the title company's escrow account within 3 business days of acceptance, and Jordan never keeps a check overnight. Escalation clauses top out at list price plus 5%, and only after Jordan has talked it through with the buyer.
