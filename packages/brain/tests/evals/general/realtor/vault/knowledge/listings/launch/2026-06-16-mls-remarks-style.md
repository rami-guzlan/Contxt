The MLS public remarks field cuts off at 1,000 characters, so Jordan keeps them under 950. The first sentence names the single strongest feature in concrete terms ("west deck over the marsh, sun on it until 8pm in July"), then the layout, updates with their years, and the lot size in acres. Square footage comes from the appraiser's sketch or Kim's laser measure, never the tax card. Banned: nestled, dream home, must see, won't last, cozy, pride of ownership, and "charming" anywhere outside the historic district.
