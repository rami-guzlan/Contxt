Marcus Bell runs Sandwort Inspection Co and is the first inspector Jordan suggests. Prices are $475 for homes under 2,000 square feet and $575 above, plus $185 for a sewer scope and $150 for a radon test. From June to September he books 3 to 4 days out, so Kim calls him the same day an offer is accepted. His PDF report arrives within 24 hours and Jordan reads it before the buyer call.
