Roughly 40% of Jordan's business is first-time buyers, 30% is local owners moving to something smaller, and the rest is second-home buyers who drive in from the city about 2.5 hours inland. Jordan does not take rentals, commercial property or land over 5 acres. Rental inquiries go to Pat Lindqvist at Driftrow Rentals and commercial ones to Sam Whitford in the same office; Jordan gets a 25% referral fee on both.
