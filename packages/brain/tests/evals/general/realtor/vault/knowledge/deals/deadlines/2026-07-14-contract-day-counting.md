On the state purchase agreement Jordan uses (form PSA-12), every period is counted in calendar days starting the day after acceptance. A deadline ends at 5:00pm local time on its last day. If that day is a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday, it moves to the next business day. The acceptance date is when the last party signs and the signed copy is delivered, not when the offer was written.
