Write "primary bedroom", not "master". Write "0.4 miles to Gull Beach" instead of "walking distance". Say "first-floor bedroom with full bath" rather than "perfect for empty nesters" or "great for families", and "quiet dead-end street" rather than "safe neighborhood". Ellen Ruiz, the managing broker, reviews any ad copy Jordan is unsure about, usually the same day.
