Every seller completes and signs the Seller Property Condition Statement before the listing goes live, and Kim attaches it to the MLS entry. Jordan never fills it in for the seller, even when asked to. If Jordan knows of a material defect (water in the basement after a storm, a failed septic test), buyers must be told even if the seller leaves it off; Jordan will give up the listing rather than hide it.
