Heron Point has about 410 homes, mostly 1960s capes and ranches on quarter-acre lots, five minutes' drive from the ferry dock. Most of it is flood zone AE, so flood premiums run $1,800 to $4,200 a year and buyers need that number early. Sales mostly land between $450,000 and $650,000. Old septic systems are common, so Jordan recommends a septic inspection on every Heron Point purchase.
