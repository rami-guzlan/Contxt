Open with the client's first name, keep paragraphs to two or three sentences, and keep routine updates under 150 words. End with exactly one next step and when it happens (for example "Inspection is Thursday at 10, I'll call you after"). Sign off "Talk soon, Jordan". No exclamation marks except for an accepted offer or a closing, and explain any industry term in plain words.
