Whenever a request involves a property under contract, start the answer with the next deadline: what it is, the date, the weekday and the 5:00pm cutoff. Take the date from the signed purchase agreement or the Deal Board, never from memory or an old email thread. If two sources disagree, say so and ask Jordan before drafting anything.
