Decided 2026-06-15 after the managing broker flagged them as a fair housing risk: Jordan's buyers do not attach personal letters or family photos to their offers. On Jordan's own listings, any letter that arrives with an offer goes back to the buyer's agent unread by the seller. The offer summary Jordan gives a seller covers price and terms only.
