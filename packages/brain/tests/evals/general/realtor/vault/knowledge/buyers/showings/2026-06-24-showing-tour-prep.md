Plan no more than five homes in one outing, 30 minutes inside each plus drive time, starting with the one farthest away. Kim requests every appointment through the brokerage scheduler at least 24 hours ahead and confirms how to get in. Print a one-page sheet per home with price, taxes, flood zone and the seller's condition statement. Text the buyer the route and start time the evening before.
