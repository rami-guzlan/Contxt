Decided 2026-08-19 after a tense sale in July where both parties called Jordan about the same repair: Jordan no longer acts for both buyer and seller on one property. If a buyer without an agent wants one of Jordan's listings, Jordan refers them to Sam Whitford at Wrenhaven, who represents them on his own. Jordan stays the seller's agent only and does not advise that buyer.
