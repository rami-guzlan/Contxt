# saas-support

Priya Raghavan leads customer support at Tallyline, a B2B invoicing software company with about 600 business customers, a support team of six and an on-call engineering rotation. She uses an AI assistant to draft customer replies, follow escalation and refund policies, write help-center articles, summarize incidents, prepare the weekly support metrics, onboard new agents, and coordinate with product on bug priorities.
