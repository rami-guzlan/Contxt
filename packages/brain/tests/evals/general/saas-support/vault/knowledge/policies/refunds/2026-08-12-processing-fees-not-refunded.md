Decided 2026-08-12 with Gareth Nyland (finance): the Tallyline Pay processing fee (2.9% plus 30 cents per card payment a customer collects) is not refunded, even when we refund the subscription. The only exception is a duplicate charge caused by a Tallyline bug, and then the fee comes back as account credit, not cash. Before this, agents decided case by case and customers got different answers.
