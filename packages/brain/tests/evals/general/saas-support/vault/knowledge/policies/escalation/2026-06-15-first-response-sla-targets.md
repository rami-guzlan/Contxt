First response targets in business hours: Scale 1 hour, Growth 4 hours, Starter 1 business day. Chat should be picked up within 2 minutes. A Sev 1 from any plan gets a reply within 30 minutes, any time of day, through on-call. The clock pauses while a ticket waits on the customer.
