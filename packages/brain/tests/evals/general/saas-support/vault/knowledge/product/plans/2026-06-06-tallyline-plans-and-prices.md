Starter $29 a month: 1 user, 50 invoices a month, no Tallyline Pay. Growth $79 a month: 5 users, unlimited invoices, Tallyline Pay and bank feeds, extra users $12 each. Scale $199 a month: unlimited users, accounting sync, 1 hour first response and a named support contact. Annual billing gives 2 months free.
