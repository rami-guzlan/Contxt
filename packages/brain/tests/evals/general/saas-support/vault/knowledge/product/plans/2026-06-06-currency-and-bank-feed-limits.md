Multi-currency invoicing is on Growth and Scale only, with 24 currencies. Tallyline Pay pays out in USD, CAD or EUR only. A recurring invoice cannot change currency once it is created; the customer has to set up a new schedule. Bank feeds connect to US and Canadian banks only.
