Day 1: helpdesk login, read-only billing console, the support channels, and read the 20 most viewed help articles. Days 2 and 3: shadow Marisol on billing tickets in the morning and Rosa on chat in the afternoon. Day 4: work tier 1 tickets in draft mode, with Marisol reviewing each reply before it goes. Day 5: 30 minutes with Priya to walk through escalation, refunds and the admin check, then fill in the week one checklist in the team drive.
