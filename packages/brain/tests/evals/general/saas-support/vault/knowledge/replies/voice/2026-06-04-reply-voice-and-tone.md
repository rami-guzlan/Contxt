Answer the actual question in the first sentence, then the steps. Numbered steps for anything over two clicks, and short paragraphs. Apologize once and say what for; never blame the customer or the system. No exclamation marks, no emojis, no Hope this helps. Sign off with the agent's first name, then Tallyline Support on the next line.
