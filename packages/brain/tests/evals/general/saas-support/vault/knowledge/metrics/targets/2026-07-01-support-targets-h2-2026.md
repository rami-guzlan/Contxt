Targets for July to December 2026: CSAT 92% or higher, median time to resolution under 18 business hours, first response SLA hit 95%, reopen rate under 6%, and under 40 tickets per 100 customers a week. CSAT is one question on a 1 to 5 scale sent 24 hours after a ticket is solved; only 4s and 5s count as satisfied.
