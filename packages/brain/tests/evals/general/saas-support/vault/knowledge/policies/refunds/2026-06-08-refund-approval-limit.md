Agents can approve refunds and account credits up to $500 per customer in any 12 months, on monthly plans only. Above that, any annual plan refund, and any request to refund to a card or bank account other than the one that paid all need Priya's approval in a ticket internal note first. Never tell a customer a refund is coming before that note exists. Gareth Nyland in finance issues the money; we say it lands 5 to 10 business days after it is issued, never a specific date.
