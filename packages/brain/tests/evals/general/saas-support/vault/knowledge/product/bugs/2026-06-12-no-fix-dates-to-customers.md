summary: Customers never get a date or release for a fix or feature, even when engineering has one internally.

status: approved

added: 2026-06-12

supersedes: none
startup_text: Never give a customer a date or release for a bug fix or feature; say it is logged with engineering and that we will update them.
---
Do not tell customers when a bug will be fixed or a feature will ship, even if engineering shared a sprint or a date internally. Say it is logged, that we passed on how it affects them, and that the ticket stays open until there is news. Only Leah or Priya can give a date, and only in writing.
