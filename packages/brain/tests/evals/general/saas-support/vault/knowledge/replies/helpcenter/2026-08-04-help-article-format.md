Titles start with a verb (Reconnect a bank feed). One task per article, under 600 words. Order: one line on what it does, who can do it (plan and role), numbered steps with exact button names in bold, then a section called If it still does not work. Screenshots only from the demo account Juniper Ridge Bakery, never a real customer's screen. Last updated date at the bottom.
