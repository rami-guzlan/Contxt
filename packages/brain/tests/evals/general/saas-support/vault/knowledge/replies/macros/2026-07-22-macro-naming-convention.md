Name format is the area in capitals, a colon, then the action; the areas are BILLING, BANK, INVOICE, ACCOUNT, INCIDENT, ESCALATE and PRIVACY. Each macro lists its owner and a last reviewed date in the description field. Anything not reviewed for 6 months comes up at the quarterly macro cleanup. No customer names, amounts or links to a specific account inside a macro.
