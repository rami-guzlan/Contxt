Tallyline sells invoicing software to small and mid-sized businesses: invoices, recurring billing, card and bank payments through Tallyline Pay, bank feeds and accounting sync. About 600 paying business customers, most on Growth; the roughly 40 Scale accounts bring about 30% of revenue. Around 85 staff, and support sits in Customer Operations under Daniela Sousa.
