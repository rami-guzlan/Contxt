Monthly plans: full refund of the most recent charge if asked within 14 days of it, nothing older. Annual plans: prorated refund for unused whole months if cancelled in the first 60 days; after day 60 the plan runs to the end of its term. Downgrades start at the next billing date, never mid-cycle. Refunds always go back to the original payment method.
