Came to Tallyline from running support at a payroll company, so sloppy answers about money bother her more than anything. She measures herself on fewer repeat contacts, not raw speed: one complete answer in 4 hours beats three half answers in 20 minutes. Hates replies that paste a macro without reading the question. Anything sent to her should put the ask or the decision needed in the first line, then bullets.
