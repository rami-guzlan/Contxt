P0 fix now (goes with a Sev 1), P1 this sprint, P2 within the next two sprints, P3 backlog. Support can suggest a priority, but Leah Castellano, product manager for billing and bank feeds, sets it. Every bug support raises fills in the customer-impact field: number of accounts affected and their combined monthly revenue.
