Helpdesk: Quillcase until the cutover on 2026-11-02, then Fernhelp. Paging: PageRelay, service tallyline-core. Billing admin console for plans, refunds and credits (read-only for agents until signed off). The public status page is edited by support. The CSAT survey tool sends one question 24 hours after a ticket is solved, and the team drive holds the weekly reports, the triage doc and the onboarding checklists.
