1. Verify the requester is the account owner (the admin check).
2. Tag the ticket data-deletion and forward it to Noor Haddad, privacy lead, the same day.
3. Noor confirms the scope and engineering runs the deletion job.
4. Confirm to the customer in writing within 30 days of the request with the PRIVACY: Deletion confirmed macro.
Invoices we must keep for tax (7 years) are kept, and the reply says so.
