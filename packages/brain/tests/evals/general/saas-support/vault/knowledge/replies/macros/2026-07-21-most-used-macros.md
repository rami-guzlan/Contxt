Most used: BILLING: Refund issued; BANK: Feed reconnect (about 18% of all tickets end with it); INVOICE: Email bounced; ESCALATE: Tier 2; PRIVACY: Deletion confirmed; INCIDENT: Sev 1 holding reply. The holding reply links the status page and names the time of the next update. Marisol keeps the master list.
