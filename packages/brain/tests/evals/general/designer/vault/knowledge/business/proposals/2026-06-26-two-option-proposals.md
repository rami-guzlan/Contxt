On 26 June 2026 Mia dropped three-tier proposals (bronze, silver, gold): clients kept choosing the middle one and then asking for gold extras for free. Every proposal now has exactly two options. Essentials is the core job; Complete adds the style guide document, a starter set of social templates and an extra review cycle, and is usually priced 35 to 45 percent above Essentials.
