Quotes are always a fixed fee per project or per stage, built from the current rate card, never an hourly estimate. Don't offer discounts, free extras or friend prices on your own. If a client says it is too expensive, suggest a smaller scope and flag it to Mia. Charities and arts groups can get 10 percent off, but only when Mia says so.
