On 20 August 2026 Mia and Ingrid agreed a monthly retainer starting 1 September: EUR 900 excl. VAT for up to 10 hours of design (market signage, product cards, web banners, mailing graphics). Unused hours do not roll over and extra hours are billed at EUR 90. Either side can end it with one month's notice. It is invoiced on the 1st of each month.
