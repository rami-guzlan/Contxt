Orchard Row's palette: Flour #F6F1E7 for backgrounds, Rye #5B3A29 for text, Crust #D9822B for accents only (never more than about 10 percent of a layout). Headings in Harrow Serif, body in Pell Sans, both licensed in Orchard Row's name. The strapline "baked on the row since 1987" is always lowercase. Clear space around the logo equals the height of the O in Orchard.
