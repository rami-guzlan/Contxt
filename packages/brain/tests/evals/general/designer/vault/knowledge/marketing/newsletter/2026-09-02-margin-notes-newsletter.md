Mia's newsletter is Margin Notes. It goes out on the first Tuesday of each month at 8:00 to about 1,150 subscribers, mostly designers and small business owners plus a few past clients. Each issue has one short essay on a single idea, three links worth reading and a two-line studio update, 500 words at most. Opens sit around 52 percent. The draft is due to Mia the Friday before.
