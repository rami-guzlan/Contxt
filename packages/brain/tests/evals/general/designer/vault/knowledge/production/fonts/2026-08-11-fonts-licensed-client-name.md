Mia's own font licences cover her studio, not her clients. Before a typeface goes into a client's website, documents or templates, check that the client holds a licence for that use and traffic level, bought in their name, with the licence file saved in 05_Handoff. If there isn't one, stop and add the licence to the quote as a pass-through cost. Free fonts are fine only when their licence allows commercial use and web embedding.
