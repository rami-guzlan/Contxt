1. Check the written go-ahead is on file. 2. Draft four parts: the problem in the client's words, what Mia did and why, what changed (one real number, such as online bookings up 30 percent), and a short client quote. 3. Keep it to 600 to 900 words with six to eight images, the lead image cropped 3:2. 4. Send the quote and any figures to the client to approve. 5. Publish on a Friday and feature it in the next Margin Notes.
