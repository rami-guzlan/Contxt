If a client cancels before concepts are shown, Mia keeps the 40 percent deposit and nothing more is owed. After concepts are presented the kill fee is 50 percent of the total; after design approval it is 80 percent. A project the client pauses for more than 60 days counts as cancelled unless both sides agree a new start date in writing.
