Ingrid Halden runs Halden Ceramics, a two-person pottery selling online and at weekend markets. The wordmark is always lowercase "halden", set in Morrow Grotesk. Product photos are shot on raw linen in natural daylight, never on a white sweep. No drop shadows, gradients or rounded buttons, and one accent colour, Kiln Red #A4402A. Ingrid sends comments as voice notes, so write them out as a numbered list before acting on them.
