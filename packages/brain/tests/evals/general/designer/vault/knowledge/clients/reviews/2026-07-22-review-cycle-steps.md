1. Send one review link with three to five numbered questions (never just "what do you think?"). 2. Give five working days and name the date. 3. Ask for a single merged reply from the person who decides, not separate notes from the whole team. 4. Sort the comments into "will do", "suggest otherwise" and "out of scope" and send that list back for a yes before Mia opens the file. 5. No reply by the date means one reminder, and after three more working days the project pauses and the slot is released.
