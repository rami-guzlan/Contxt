The numbers Mia works to: normal text contrast at least 4.5:1; large text (24px, or 19px bold) and interface icons at least 3:1. Body copy is never below 16px on the web (18px for Kestrel). Tap targets are at least 44 by 44px, line length 60 to 75 characters, line height 1.5 for body text. Orchard Row's Crust orange fails on Flour, so it is never used for text.
