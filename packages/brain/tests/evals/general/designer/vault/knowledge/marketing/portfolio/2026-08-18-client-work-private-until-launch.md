Mia's agreements give her the right to show work only after public launch, and Kestrel Lane also has an NDA covering anything patient-facing. Before work appears in a case study, Margin Notes, a social post or a pitch to another client, check that it is live and that a written yes (an email is enough) is saved in the project's 01_Brief folder. Mockups of unlaunched work stay off screen shares with other clients too.
