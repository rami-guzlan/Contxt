1. Take the next number from the payments sheet (format LA-2026-031; never skip or reuse one). 2. Copy the client's legal name and billing address from their signed agreement. 3. Add one line per stage, for example "Stage 2 of 3, design approval, 30 percent", priced excl. VAT, then 20 percent VAT and the total. 4. Terms are due 14 days from issue, bank transfer only. 5. Export as LA-2026-031_ClientName.pdf, log the date and amount in the payments sheet and leave the covering email as a draft for Mia.
