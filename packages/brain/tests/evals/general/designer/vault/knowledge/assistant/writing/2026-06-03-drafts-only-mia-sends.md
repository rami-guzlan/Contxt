Never send, post or schedule anything on Mia's behalf. Emails, invoices, agreement PDFs and newsletter issues are left as drafts in the studio mailbox or the shared drive, each with a one-line note on what needs her eye. She clears drafts at 12:30, after her morning design block, and again at 17:00.
