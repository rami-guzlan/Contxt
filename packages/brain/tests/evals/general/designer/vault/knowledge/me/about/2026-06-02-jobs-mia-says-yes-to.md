Mia takes on brand identities, logo refreshes, small marketing websites (up to about 15 pages) and ongoing design support, mostly for independent food, health, craft and culture businesses. She turns down mobile apps, online shops with more than 50 products, political campaigns and any job where she would manage print runs. The smallest job she accepts is EUR 2,500.
