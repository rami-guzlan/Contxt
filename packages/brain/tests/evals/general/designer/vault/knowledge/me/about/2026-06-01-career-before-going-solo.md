Mia studied graphic design with a typography major, then spent six years at Atelier Corvel, a mid-sized Lyon branding agency (closed in 2021), ending as senior designer on hospitality and retail accounts. She went independent in March 2019. Her favourite work is type-led identities and wordmarks; for illustration-heavy briefs she brings in a freelance illustrator rather than drawing herself.
