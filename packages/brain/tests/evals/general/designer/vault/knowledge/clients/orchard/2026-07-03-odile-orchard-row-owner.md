Odile Marchand owns Orchard Row's three bakeries and is the only person who can approve design. She hates long email threads: put the question in the first line, or ring her on a Tuesday afternoon when the shops are quiet. Her shop manager Bastien Roux handles signage and print orders but cannot approve anything. Invoices go to their bookkeeper, Claire Vidal, not to Odile.
