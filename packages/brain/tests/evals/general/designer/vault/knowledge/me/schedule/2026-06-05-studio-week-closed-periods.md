Mia designs Monday to Thursday and keeps Friday for admin, billing, the newsletter and portfolio writing. The studio is closed 3 to 21 August, 22 December to 4 January, and on French public holidays. Don't let a client deadline land on a Friday or in the first week back after a closure.
