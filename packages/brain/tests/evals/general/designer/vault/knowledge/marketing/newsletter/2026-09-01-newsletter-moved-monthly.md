Decided 1 September 2026: Margin Notes switched from fortnightly to monthly. Writing every other Friday was eating the time meant for case studies and admin, and unsubscribes crept up after each send. October was the first monthly issue. An occasional extra issue for a big launch is possible, but only Mia decides that.
