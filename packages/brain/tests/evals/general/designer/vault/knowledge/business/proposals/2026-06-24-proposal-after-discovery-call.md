The proposal goes out within two working days of the discovery call, as a PDF of four pages at most. Order: a paragraph playing back what the client said in their own words, their goals, what is in and out of scope, the two options with fixed fees, a week-by-week timeline, the payment stages, and one next step (sign and pay the deposit to secure the start date). A start date is held for 10 working days only.
