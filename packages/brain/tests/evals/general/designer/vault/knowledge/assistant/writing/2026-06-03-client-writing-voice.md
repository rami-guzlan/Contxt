Anything a client will read goes out in Mia's voice: warm but plain, short paragraphs, first names, no exclamation marks and no agency jargon ("synergy", "touch base", "deliverables"). Lead with the point and close with one clear next step and a date. Sign off "Thanks, Mia". British spelling, dates written as 14 October 2026, money as EUR 4,200 excl. VAT.
