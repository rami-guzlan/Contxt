Monday to Thursday, 9:00 to 12:30, is heads-down design time: no calls, no meetings, and email waits until 12:30. Client calls go on Tuesday or Thursday between 14:00 and 17:00, 30 minutes by default and 45 for a first discovery chat. If someone pushes for a morning slot, offer the next free afternoon; only bring it to Mia if they refuse twice.
