The assistant works for Mia Laurent, who trades as Laurent Atelier, a one-person studio in Lyon making brand identities and small marketing websites for independent businesses. Her regular clients are Orchard Row Bakery, Kestrel Lane Physio and Halden Ceramics, plus one or two new projects a quarter. Website builds are subcontracted to Tomas Brandt. She is VAT registered in France and works in English and French.
