Tomas starts a build only after the client has approved the designs. The 05_Handoff folder holds approved desktop and mobile designs, a one-page spec (type scale, 8px spacing unit, colour values, breakpoints at 640, 1024 and 1440px), icons as SVG and photos as WebP, the final copy, and the font licence files. Book a 30-minute walkthrough call with him the same week.
