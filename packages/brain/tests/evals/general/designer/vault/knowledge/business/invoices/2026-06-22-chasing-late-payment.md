Day 3 past due: a short, friendly reminder with the invoice attached again. Day 14: a firmer email mentioning the EUR 40 recovery charge and that interest now applies. Day 30: Mia phones the client herself and all work on their files stops until the money arrives. Log every chase with its date in the payments sheet. Halden pays by standing order, so check the bank feed before chasing them.
