Every agreement splits the fee in three: 40 percent deposit before work starts, 30 percent when the client approves the design, 30 percent at launch or file handover. Invoices are due within 14 days, and late payment carries the French fixed EUR 40 recovery charge plus interest. Usage rights and final files pass to the client only once the last invoice is paid; Mia keeps the right to show the work after launch.
