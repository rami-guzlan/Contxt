From recent jobs: a web licence for one family up to 50,000 monthly pageviews costs about EUR 250 to 400, and a desktop plus web bundle for a small team EUR 600 to 900. Fonts go in proposals as a separate pass-through line at cost, with no markup, and where possible the client pays the foundry directly. Orchard Row's Harrow Serif and Pell Sans came to EUR 780 together in 2025.
