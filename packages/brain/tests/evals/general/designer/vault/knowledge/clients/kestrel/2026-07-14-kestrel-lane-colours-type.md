Kestrel Lane uses Deep Teal #0F5257 and Warm Sand #E8DCC4 with white, and a single typeface, Calder Sans, in regular and semibold only. Body text on screen is never below 18px because many patients are over 60. Photos show the real staff and treatment rooms; the partners banned stock images of athletes in the 2025 brief.
