Orchard Row's old wheat-sheaf logo was retired with the rebrand Mia delivered in April 2025. Don't use it anywhere, including loyalty cards, stickers and social avatars, and replace it whenever it turns up in a file someone sends over. The only approved marks are the full wordmark and the round OR stamp.
