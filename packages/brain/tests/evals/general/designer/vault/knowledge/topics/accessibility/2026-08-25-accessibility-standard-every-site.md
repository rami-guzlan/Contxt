All websites meet WCAG 2.2 level AA, and that promise is written into every agreement. Contrast, focus states, text sizes and alt text are checked in the design files before handoff, not left for Tomas to patch in the build. If a client's brand colours fail contrast, propose a darker text shade rather than quietly changing their palette.
