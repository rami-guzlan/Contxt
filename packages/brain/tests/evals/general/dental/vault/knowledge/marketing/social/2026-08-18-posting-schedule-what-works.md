We post twice a week, Tuesday around 7:30 pm and Friday at noon. Biscuit posts get roughly three times the likes of anything else, followed by quick tips under 60 words and team birthdays. Before-and-after photos and long captions do worst. Captions are drafted a week ahead and Patrice schedules them.
