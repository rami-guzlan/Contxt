Thank every reviewer by first name or handle in two or three sentences. Never confirm or hint that the reviewer is our patient, and never mention a visit, a treatment or a bill in a public reply, even to set the record straight. For an unhappy review, write a short apology that they had a poor experience and ask them to call Patrice at 555-0142. I approve every reply to a negative review before it goes up.
