For anxious patients we offer nitrous oxide ($45 per visit, not covered by most plans), noise-cancelling headphones, a weighted blanket, and a hello from Biscuit before they are seated. Anyone who uses one of these gets a COMFORT PLAN note in their chart so we set it up before they arrive. We agree on a raised hand as the signal to stop, and we always honor it.
