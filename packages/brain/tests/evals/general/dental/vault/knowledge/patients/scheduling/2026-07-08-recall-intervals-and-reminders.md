Most adults and kids are on a six-month recall; perio maintenance patients come back every three months, and most of those are Leo's. Reminder texts go out three weeks before and again two days before; patients without a mobile number get a postcard four weeks ahead. Anyone more than 18 months overdue goes on Patrice's quarterly call list.
