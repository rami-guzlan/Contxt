When something is for me rather than a patient, put the answer or the decision I need to make in the first line, then no more than five bullets. Show money with cents ($1,280.00) and dates as weekday plus date (Wed Oct 21). I read messages between patients, so anything that needs me today goes at the very top marked NEEDS NADIA.
