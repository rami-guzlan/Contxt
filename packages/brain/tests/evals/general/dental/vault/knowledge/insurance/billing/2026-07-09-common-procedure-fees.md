Our 2026 fees for what people ask about most: adult cleaning $135, child cleaning $95, periodic exam $62, new patient exam $105, four bitewing X-rays $74, full set of X-rays $148, one-surface tooth-colored filling $190, porcelain crown $1,280, emergency exam $89. Deep cleaning (gum treatment) is $265 per quarter of the mouth. Fees are reviewed every January.
