For the hard ones (an overdue balance, a patient turning down treatment they need, a complaint about how something turned out) we never hash it out by text. First, Patrice offers a phone call or a 15-minute sit-down with me within one business day. On the call I listen first, then explain the options and what happens if we wait, and I write a short note in the chart afterwards. If they still refuse treatment, they sign the informed refusal form and we keep the door open.
