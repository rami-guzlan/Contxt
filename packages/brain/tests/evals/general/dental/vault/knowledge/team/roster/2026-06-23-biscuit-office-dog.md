Biscuit is my seven-year-old cream goldendoodle, a certified therapy dog, and he comes in on Tuesdays and Thursdays only. He stays behind the baby gate in the back office unless a patient asks to see him. Patients with dog allergies or a fear of dogs have a NO BISCUIT flag in their chart, and he is shut in my office during their visit. He is never allowed in the operatories or the sterilization room.
