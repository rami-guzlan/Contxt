Brightwater Family Dental is a three-operatory general practice at 41 Alder Street, upstairs from the pharmacy. We have roughly 1,850 active patients, about a third of them kids; we see children from their first tooth onward and refer out braces and wisdom teeth. Operatory 1 is mine, 2 and 3 are the hygiene rooms. The main line is 555-0142.
