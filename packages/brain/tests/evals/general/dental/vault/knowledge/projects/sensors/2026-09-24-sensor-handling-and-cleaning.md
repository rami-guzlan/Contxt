1. Put a fresh barrier sleeve on the sensor for every patient, plus a finger cot over the cable end.
2. Between patients, wipe it with the gray-top disinfectant wipes and let it air dry; never autoclave it, soak it or bend the cable.
3. Position it in the mouth by the holder, never by the cable, and hang it on the operatory hook rather than putting it in a drawer.
4. If a sensor is dropped, test it on the practice phantom before the next patient and tell Marisol the same day.
