We hold two emergency slots each day, 11:00 and 3:30, and release them to the waitlist at 9:00 that morning if nobody has claimed them. When someone calls in pain, ask about swelling, fever, a knocked-out or broken tooth, and how long it has been going on. Swelling that spreads toward the eye or makes it hard to breathe or swallow means the hospital ER right now, not us. A knocked-out adult tooth goes in a cup of milk and the patient comes straight in, bumping whoever holds the slot. The emergency exam is $89 plus any X-rays.
