On 2026-06-26 I decided to leave the Pellham Dental HMO network, effective September 1. Their capitation worked out to about 40 percent of our usual fees, and claims took around 70 days to pay. The 63 patients on that plan got a letter in July offering to stay with us out of network or join the Smile Club, and Patrice keeps track of who chose what.
