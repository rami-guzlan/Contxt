Decided on 2026-09-10: anyone working a Saturday clinic gets their regular hourly rate plus $7 an hour. Saturdays are voluntary and people sign up a month ahead, with nobody working more than two Saturdays a month. We will look at the premium again in March 2027, once we know whether Saturdays pay for themselves.
