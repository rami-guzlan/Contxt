Patients can have copies of their X-rays and chart notes free of charge, and we send them within 10 business days. A signed release is needed before anything goes to another dentist, a specialist or a family member; the release forms are in the top drawer of the front desk. Records go out through the portal or on an encrypted USB stick, never as plain email attachments.
