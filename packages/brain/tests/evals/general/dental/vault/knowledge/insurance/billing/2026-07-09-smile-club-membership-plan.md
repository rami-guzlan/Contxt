The Brightwater Smile Club is for patients without dental insurance. Adults pay $32 a month and kids $24, on a 12-month term. It covers two cleanings, two exams, the X-rays we need that year and one emergency exam, plus 15 percent off everything else. It is not insurance and cannot be combined with a dental plan; Patrice signs people up at checkout.
