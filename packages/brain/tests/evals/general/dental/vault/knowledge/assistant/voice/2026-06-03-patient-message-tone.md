Anything going to a patient should read like a kind neighbor wrote it: short sentences, no clinical jargon (say "gum treatment", not "scaling and root planing"), and under about 120 words. Sign off as "The Brightwater team", not with my name, unless I say otherwise. No exclamation marks and no emoji. Use the first name for adults who have been with us a while, and Mr. or Ms. plus last name for new patients and anyone over 70.
