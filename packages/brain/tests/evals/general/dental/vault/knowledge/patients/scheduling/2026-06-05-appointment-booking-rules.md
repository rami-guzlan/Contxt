New patient visits get 90 minutes and only go in the 8:00 or 9:30 slot. Crown preps and extractions are mine on Tuesday and Thursday mornings only, and never back to back. Children under 6 are booked before 11:00 because afternoon visits with little ones go badly. A standard cleaning is 60 minutes; perio maintenance is 75.
