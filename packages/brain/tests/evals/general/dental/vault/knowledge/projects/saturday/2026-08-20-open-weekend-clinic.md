Decided 2026-08-20: we will open on the first and third Saturday of each month, 8:00 to 1:00, rather than the late Wednesday evenings we also looked at. The patient survey (212 replies) preferred Saturday mornings two to one, and evenings would have meant paying for a second front desk shift. Saturdays are for cleanings and emergencies only, no crowns or surgery.
