Anyone with a fever or a stomach bug stays home, no exceptions. If a hygienist calls out, Patrice first asks the other hygienist about extra hours, then calls Tallow Creek Temps, which charges $64 an hour with a four-hour minimum. If no temp can come, Patrice reschedules that hygienist's column and gives each bumped patient priority on the waitlist.
