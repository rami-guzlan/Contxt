At every visit, the patient reviews their health history on the front desk tablet before they are seated, even if they say nothing has changed. New medications, pregnancy, recent surgery or a new diagnosis get typed into the chart alert box, and the hygienist tells me before starting. A complete new form is signed once a year, in the patient's birthday month.
