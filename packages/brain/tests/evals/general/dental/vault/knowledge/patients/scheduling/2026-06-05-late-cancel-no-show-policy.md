We ask for 48 hours notice to move or cancel a visit. The first missed visit in a year is a free pass; after that it is a $50 fee for each no-show or late cancel. Three misses in 12 months moves the patient to same-day booking only. Patrice can waive one fee per patient per year without asking me.
