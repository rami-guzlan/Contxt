Decided 2026-07-29 to retire our 2016 wired sensors and buy three Ferncliff FX-2 sensors (two size 2 and one size 1 for kids). The total is $19,400 after a $1,800 trade-in credit for the old set. The old ones kept cracking at the cable, and each replacement cable was $1,150. Ferncliff's five-year warranty covers one accidental drop per sensor.
