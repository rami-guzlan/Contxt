Posts are friendly and useful, never salesy. No superlatives or promises like "best dentist in town", "painless" or "guaranteed", since the dental board frowns on them. No patient faces, smiles or names unless their media consent is on file, and staff appear only if they said yes that week. Prices and discounts never go in a post; point people to call the office instead.
