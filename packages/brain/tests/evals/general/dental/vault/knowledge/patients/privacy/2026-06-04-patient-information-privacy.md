Patient details stay inside the chart and the secure patient portal. That means no names or tooth details in public posts, in replies to online reviews, in staff group chats, or in regular email, even when the patient brought it up first. A smile photo or a patient's face can be shared only when a signed media consent form is scanned into their chart and covers that specific use. When in doubt, leave the person out and ask me.
