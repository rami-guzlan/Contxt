We are in network with Bellmont Dental PPO, Tidewell Benefits, Northgate Dental Plus and the state children's dental program (kids only). Everything else is out of network: we still file the claim as a courtesy, but the patient pays us at the visit and the insurer reimburses them. Patrice checks eligibility and the remaining annual maximum three business days before each appointment and notes it on the schedule.
