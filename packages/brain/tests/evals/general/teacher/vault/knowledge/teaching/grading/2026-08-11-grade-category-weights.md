The science department uses the same gradebook weights in both grades: tests and quizzes 40%, labs 30%, classwork 20%, homework 10%. The first quarter closes October 16, 2026 and the first semester closes January 15, 2027. Students may retake a test (not a quiz) within one week, and the higher score counts up to 85%.
