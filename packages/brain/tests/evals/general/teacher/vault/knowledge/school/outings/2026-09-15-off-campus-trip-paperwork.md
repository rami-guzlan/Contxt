1. Six weeks before, send the trip request form to vice principal Marcus Lindqvist with the date, destination, cost per student and the standards it covers. 2. Get a bus quote from district transportation and attach it. 3. Three weeks before, send permission slips home in English and Spanish; no signed slip means the student stays at school. 4. Line up one chaperone per ten students, each with a cleared district background check. 5. One week before, get the medication and allergy list from nurse Ben Adeyemi, and carry the emergency contact binder on the day.
