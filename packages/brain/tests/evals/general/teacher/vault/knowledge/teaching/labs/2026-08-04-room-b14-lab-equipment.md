Room B14 has eight lab stations that seat four students each, so 32 at most. There are eight hot plates, 40 pairs of splash goggles in the sanitizing cabinet, two eyewash stations and one safety shower by the door. The fume hood has been out of service since May 2026, so nothing that gives off fumes can be planned until facilities repairs it. The gas valves at the stations are shut off at the main and never used.
