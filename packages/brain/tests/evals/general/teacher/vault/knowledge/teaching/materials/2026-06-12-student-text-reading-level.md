Many of Sam's students read below grade level, so worksheets, lab sheets and slides aim for about a fifth-grade reading level, even in 8th grade. Keep sentences under about 15 words, one instruction per line, and number the steps of any procedure. New science terms are bolded and defined in a word box at the top of the page, five per page at most. Every handout gets at least one labeled diagram or picture.
