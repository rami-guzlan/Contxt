As of October 8, 2026, the club has two teams entered in the Tri-County Robotics Challenge regional on Saturday February 6, 2027 at Lakeshore High School, at $75 per team from the PTO money. Team Volt has a working drivetrain and is starting on its autonomous code. Team Torque is rebuilding its claw after it cracked in testing. The first practice scrimmage is Saturday November 14.
