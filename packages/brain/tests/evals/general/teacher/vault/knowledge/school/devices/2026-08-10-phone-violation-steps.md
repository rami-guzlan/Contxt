If a phone comes out during class, the first time is a verbal warning, the second time it goes to the front office until dismissal, and the third time in a quarter a family member has to collect it in person and vice principal Marcus Lindqvist is told. Students on the nurse's medical list (for example, glucose monitoring) keep their phone on the desk. Phones may be used for lab photos only when Sam hands them out from the pouches for that purpose.
