The robot club meets Tuesdays and Thursdays from 2:50 to 4:15 pm in room B14, with no meetings during conference week. There are 18 members this year from grades 6 to 8, split into two build teams of nine. Math teacher Tom Akers co-coaches and covers Thursdays when Sam has meetings. Families pick up by 4:30, and anyone still waiting goes to the front office with a coach.
