Lab write-ups are scored on four criteria, each from 1 to 4: question and hypothesis, data table and graph, analysis, and conclusion with evidence, for 16 points in all. A 4 in analysis requires quoting real numbers from the student's own data. A 3 means the work meets the standard, and a 4 is kept for going beyond it. The score goes into the gradebook as a percentage of 16.
