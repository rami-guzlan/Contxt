Priya Raman is the special education case manager for 7th and 8th grade and the person to ask about any IEP. Counselor Alma Reyes coordinates 504 plans and is also the first call for a student in distress. Nurse Ben Adeyemi keeps the medical list, including which students may keep a phone out. Before changing how a test is given to a student with a plan, Sam checks with Priya or Alma first.
