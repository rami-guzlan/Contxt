Sam Whitaker is in a twelfth year of teaching, the last six at Ridgeview Middle School in the Cedar Valley district. Sam has two 7th grade sections and three 8th grade sections, all taught in room B14, the science lab at the end of the B hallway. On Tuesday and Thursday afternoons Sam runs the robot club with math teacher Tom Akers.
