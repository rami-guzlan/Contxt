Sam's rule of thumb is "do it, then name it": students handle the materials or watch a phenomenon first, and the vocabulary comes after. Sam would rather run a messy ten-minute investigation than a tidy slideshow. Every student in a group should touch the equipment at least once per lab, so group jobs rotate (materials, recorder, timer, reporter). Sam counts a lesson as a success when a quiet kid can explain the result out loud.
