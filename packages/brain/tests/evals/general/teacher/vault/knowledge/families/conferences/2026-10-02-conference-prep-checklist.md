1. Print the gradebook summary for the student with missing work highlighted. 2. Pull two work samples, one the student is proud of and one that shows the concern. 3. Write three talking points on an index card: a strength, one concern and the plan. 4. Check whether the student has an IEP or 504 and ask Priya Raman if anything is pending. 5. Afterward, jot down what was agreed and email the family a two-line recap within two school days.
