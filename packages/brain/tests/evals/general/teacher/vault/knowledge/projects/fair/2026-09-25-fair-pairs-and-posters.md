Decided September 25, 2026 with Helen Castellanos: students may do fair projects alone or in pairs, but not in groups of three. The required trifold board is dropped, and a poster or a five-slide deck on a school laptop now counts as the display. The reason is that boards cost families $8 to $15 and favored kids with help at home. Judges score pairs with the same guide and ask each partner one question.
