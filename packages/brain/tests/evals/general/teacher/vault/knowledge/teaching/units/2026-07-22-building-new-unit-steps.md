1. Write the end-of-unit test first: about 20 questions plus one written claim, evidence and reasoning response. 2. List the state standard codes it covers (written like 8.PS.2) and put them on the unit cover page. 3. Pick two anchor investigations that students come back to all unit. 4. Choose at most 12 vocabulary words for the whole unit. 5. Only then map the daily lessons, usually 14 to 16 class days, with a short quiz around day 8.
