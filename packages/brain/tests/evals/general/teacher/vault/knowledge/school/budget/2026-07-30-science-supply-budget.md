Sam's classroom budget for 2026-27 is $1,850 for consumables and small equipment, plus $400 from the PTO for the robot club, tracked separately. Orders go through the district purchase order system to the approved vendor, Northfork Lab Supply, and anything over $250 needs Principal Dana Okafor's signature first. The last day to place spring orders is January 15, 2027. Sam does not buy supplies out of pocket, because reimbursements take about eight weeks and need receipts within 30 days.
