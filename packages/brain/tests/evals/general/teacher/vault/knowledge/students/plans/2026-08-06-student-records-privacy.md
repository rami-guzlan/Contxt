Student records are protected by federal privacy law and district policy. In seating charts, group lists, planning notes and anything drafted for colleagues, refer to students by initials and period (for example J.M., P5). Never name another student in a message to a family, even when describing a conflict between two kids. Grades, accommodations and health details are never posted publicly or emailed to a group.
