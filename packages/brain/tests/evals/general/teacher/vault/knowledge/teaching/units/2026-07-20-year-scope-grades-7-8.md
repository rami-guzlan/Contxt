7th grade order for 2026-27: Unit 1 cells and body systems (late August to October), Unit 2 ecosystems and food webs (November to January), Unit 3 earth's surface and weather (February to April), Unit 4 review plus the watershed outing (April to May). 8th grade order: Unit 1 matter and chemical reactions (late August to October), Unit 2 forces and motion (November to January), Unit 3 waves and energy (February to March), Unit 4 genetics and heredity (April to May). Department head Helen Castellanos signed off on this sequence on July 20.
