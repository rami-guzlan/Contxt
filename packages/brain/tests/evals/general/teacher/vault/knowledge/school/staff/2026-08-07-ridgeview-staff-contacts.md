Principal Dana Okafor signs off on spending over $250 and on anything sent to all families. Vice principal Marcus Lindqvist handles discipline referrals and off-campus trips. Helen Castellanos is science department head and reviews unit plans and substitute plans. Joy Bettencourt in the front office handles copies, substitute requests and translation requests, and copy jobs are due by 2 pm for the next day. For a lab spill, call custodian Dale Pruitt at extension 214.
