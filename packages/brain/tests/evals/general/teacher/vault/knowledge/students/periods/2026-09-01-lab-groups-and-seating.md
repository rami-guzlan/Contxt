Sam always picks the lab groups, never the students: four per station, mixed by skill and reading level. Groups get reshuffled at the start of every unit. Students whose plans call for front seating stay at stations 1 and 2 near the board. A draft group list uses initials only and puts at least one strong reader in every group.
