Fall conferences run Thursday October 22 and Friday October 23, 2026, in 15-minute slots from 3:15 to 7:00 pm in room B14. Families book through the school's online sign-up, which opened October 5. Sam blocks 5:00 to 5:30 for dinner and holds two open slots each night for families who need an interpreter, so Rosa Delgado can join. Students are dismissed at noon on both days.
