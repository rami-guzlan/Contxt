Sam has a biology degree and worked four years as a field technician for a county water quality office, collecting stream samples and counting the bugs that live under rocks. Sam moved into teaching through an alternative certification program in 2015. The ecosystems unit and the spring watershed outing come straight from that background, and Sam likes being asked for real examples from the fieldwork years.
