Decided August 14, 2026: 7th grade no longer dissects preserved frogs. The body systems unit uses a free virtual dissection plus an owl pellet investigation instead. Reasons: specimens cost about $9 each, three families asked to opt out last year, and the fume hood is down. The roughly $280 the frogs would have cost went to owl pellets and replacement goggles.
