Every note home starts with one specific thing the student did well, not a generic compliment. Then raise only one concern, give one concrete next step, and offer two times Sam is free for a phone call. Keep it under 150 words, write "your student" rather than guessing who the adult is, and leave out acronyms like IEP or CER. Sam sends from the school email account only, never by text from a personal phone.
