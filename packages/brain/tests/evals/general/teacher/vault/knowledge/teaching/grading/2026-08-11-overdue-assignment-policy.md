Ridgeview's rule for 2026-27: work handed in within two school days of the due date gets full credit. After that it is capped at 70%, and nothing for a unit is accepted after that unit's test. Students whose support plan includes extended time get three extra school days with no cap. Missing work shows as M in the gradebook, which counts as a zero until something comes in.
