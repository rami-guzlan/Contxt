As of September 4, 2026, Sam's five classes include 14 students with IEPs and 9 with 504 plans, the most of any period in period 5 (seven students). The usual supports are time and a half on tests, tests read aloud, a seat near the board, and shortened assignments. Two students in period 2 use a scribe for written answers. Priya Raman keeps the current list, so check with her before assuming anything has changed.
