Decided August 10, 2026 at the opening staff meeting: from the first day of the 2026-27 year, students put phones in a numbered pouch wall by the classroom door every period. Staff voted 41 to 12 and Principal Dana Okafor made it school policy. Sam's pouch numbers match seat numbers, so collection takes under a minute at the bell. The old face-down-on-the-desk rule is gone.
