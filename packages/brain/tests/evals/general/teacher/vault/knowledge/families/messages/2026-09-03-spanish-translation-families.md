About 20 of Sam's families list Spanish as their preferred language, most of them in periods 1 and 5. Written letters are translated by the district translation service, requested through Joy Bettencourt in the front office at least three school days ahead. For phone calls, bilingual aide Rosa Delgado can join on Tuesdays and Fridays during period 3. Machine translation is not used for anything about grades or behavior.
