These come from the district safety contract plus Sam's own limits, and they apply to every lab the assistant drafts. Splash goggles stay on for any lab with liquids, heating or glassware, from setup until cleanup ends. Students never light or hold an open flame; Sam does any flame demo behind the shield. No acid or base stronger than 1 M goes to students, and every plan opens with a safety section before the materials list. A student without a signed safety contract on file does no hands-on work and gets an alternate task.
