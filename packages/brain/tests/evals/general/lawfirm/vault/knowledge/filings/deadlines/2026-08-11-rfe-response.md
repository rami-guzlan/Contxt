1. Ben scans the RFE and calendars the due date printed on it (usually 87 days from the notice date: 84 plus 3 for mail).
2. Within 3 business days Teresa sends the client a plain list of what USCIS wants and the date the firm needs it by.
3. The internal target is to mail the response by day 60, with the original RFE notice on top.
4. Ship by courier with tracking and save the delivery confirmation to the case file.
