When a client asks how long, look up the form and office on the USCIS processing times page and quote the range with the date checked ("as of August 26: 8 to 14 months"). Say plainly that cases can move faster or slower than that. If the case is already past the outer end of the range, tell Teresa, because that is when Ben can open a case inquiry.
