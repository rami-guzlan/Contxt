1. Book a 60-minute prep session about two weeks before the interview date.
2. Teresa walks through the filed N-400 line by line so the client confirms each answer is still true.
3. Ben runs the civics questions and a short reading and writing drill.
4. Go over trips abroad and tax filings, since officers dig there most.
5. The client leaves with a one-page bring list: green card, passport, state ID, interview notice and any court records.
