On the 5th of each month Teresa and the outside bookkeeper, Dana Whitfield, do a three-way reconciliation of the trust account: bank statement, trust ledger and the total of all client balances must agree to the cent. Dana charges $180 a month. Any difference is fixed and written up before the 10th, and the reports are kept for six years.
