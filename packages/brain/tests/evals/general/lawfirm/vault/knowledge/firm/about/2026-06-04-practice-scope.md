In scope: family-based petitions and green cards, H-1B work for a few repeat employers, naturalization, and asylum (affirmative, or defensive when the person is not detained). Out of scope: anything with a criminal charge (send to Marcus Feld, who does crimmigration), detained removal defense and investor visas. When the firm turns someone away, give two referral names and the state bar's lawyer referral line, never just a no.
