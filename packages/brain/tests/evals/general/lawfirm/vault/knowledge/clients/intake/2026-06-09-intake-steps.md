1. Before booking, Ben runs a conflict check in DocketLeaf on every name involved (client, spouse, petitioner, employer).
2. Book the consult and take the fee by card at booking.
3. Send the intake questionnaire in English or Spanish at least two days ahead, and book Lucia if the person prefers Spanish.
4. Within one business day after the consult, send the document checklist for that case type.
5. If they hire the firm, the engagement letter goes out for e-signature, and the file opens only once it is signed and the first payment has cleared.
