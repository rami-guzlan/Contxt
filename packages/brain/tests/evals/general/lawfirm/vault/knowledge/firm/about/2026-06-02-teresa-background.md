Teresa was admitted to the state bar in 2011 and spent six years at the Eastbrook Newcomer Legal Center, a nonprofit, mostly on asylum and family cases. She opened her own office in March 2019. Her Spanish is fine for small talk but not for explaining legal options, so Lucia sits in whenever a Spanish-speaking client needs real advice. She teaches one continuing education session a year for the state bar's immigration section, usually in April.
