The free citizenship clinic runs on the second Saturday of February, May, August and November, 10 a.m. to 1 p.m., in the Maple Room of the Eastbrook Public Library. Each attendee gets a 15-minute naturalization eligibility screening and a fee waiver check. Sign-ups come through the form linked in Puentes, and walk-ins are taken if slots remain. Lucia works every clinic at her usual rate.
