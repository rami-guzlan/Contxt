The I-589 has to reach USCIS within one year of the client's last arrival, and missing it usually ends the claim outside narrow exceptions. At intake Ben records the arrival date and calendars the one-year date in DocketLeaf. Anyone ten months or more past arrival gets a same-day flag to Teresa and jumps ahead of every other drafting job. If the arrival date is uncertain, work from the earliest date it could be.
