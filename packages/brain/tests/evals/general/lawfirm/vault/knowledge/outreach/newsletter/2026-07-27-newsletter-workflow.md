1. The assistant drafts the English issue by the 20th from Teresa's topic note.
2. Lucia returns the Spanish version by the 25th.
3. Teresa reads both and approves by the 28th; nothing about a policy change goes in until she has checked the source.
4. Ben loads it into the email tool, schedules it for the first Tuesday at 7 a.m. and sends a test copy to the office inbox.
