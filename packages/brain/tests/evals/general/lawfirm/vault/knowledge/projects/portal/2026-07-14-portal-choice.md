On July 14, 2026 Teresa picked ClientNest as the client portal, at $89 a month for up to 300 active clients. Case updates and documents now go through the portal, and the client gets an automatic text saying a new message is waiting. Plain email is kept only for scheduling and the newsletter. Why: email threads were getting forwarded with A-numbers in them, and status calls were costing Ben about six hours a week.
