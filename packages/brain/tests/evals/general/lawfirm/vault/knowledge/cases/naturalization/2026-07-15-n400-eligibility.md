The waiting period is 5 years as a permanent resident, or 3 years for someone married to and living with the same US citizen the whole time. The N-400 can be filed up to 90 days before that anniversary; DocketLeaf's date calculator counts from the "resident since" date on the card. Any trip abroad of 6 months or longer needs Teresa's look before anyone tells the client they qualify.
