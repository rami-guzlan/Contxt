Every advance payment, including a flat fee paid before the work is done, is deposited into the firm's IOLTA trust account at Eastbrook Community Bank. Money moves to the operating account only at the earned milestones in the engagement letter, with an invoice showing what was earned. Firm bills, payroll and Lucia's invoices are never paid from trust.
