A sponsor has to show income of at least 125% of the federal poverty guideline for their household size (100% for active-duty military sponsoring a spouse or child). The chart in the I-864 binder reads $26,437 for a household of two and $33,312 for three. If the sponsor falls short, try household members on an I-864A first, then a joint sponsor. Ben swaps in the new chart each spring when the guidelines change.
