Concurrent packet for the spouse of a US citizen who entered lawfully: I-130 and I-130A, I-485, the I-864 from the petitioner (plus a joint sponsor if income falls short), I-765 for the work permit and I-131 for travel. The sealed I-693 medical goes in the same envelope; the firm sends clients to Dr. Anita Sorel at Eastbrook Family Clinic, who charges $240. Ben stacks the packet in that order with colored cover sheets between forms.
