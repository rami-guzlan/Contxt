In anything a client reads, say "green card" for lawful permanent residence, "work permit" for EAD, "travel permit" for advance parole, "receipt letter" for the I-797C, "citizenship interview" for the N-400 interview and "request for more documents" for an RFE. Put the official term in parentheses the first time only. In Spanish use "tarjeta verde" and "permiso de trabajo".
