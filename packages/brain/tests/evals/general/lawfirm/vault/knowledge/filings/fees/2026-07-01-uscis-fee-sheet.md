Ben's fee sheet as of July 1, 2026: N-400 $760 on paper or $710 online; I-130 $675 on paper or $625 online; I-485 $1,440; I-765 $260 when filed with or after an I-485; I-131 $630 with an I-485; I-589 $100 plus the $100 annual asylum fee each year the case is pending. Check the USCIS fee page the day before anything is mailed, because a wrong fee sends the whole packet back.
