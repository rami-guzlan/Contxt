The first letter to a new client opens "Dear Mr. Rivas" or "Dear Ms. Rivas", using both surnames if the client gave two at intake. Move to first names only after the client writes that way. Close with "Warm regards," then "Teresa Alvarado, Attorney at Law"; the Spanish version closes with "Atentamente,". No emojis and no exclamation marks in anything about a case.
