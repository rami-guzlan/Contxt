Puentes is the firm's free monthly email newsletter, sent the first Tuesday of each month at 7 a.m. to about 1,140 subscribers (September count). Each issue runs about 600 words in English with the Spanish version right underneath. Standing parts: one explainer, upcoming clinic dates, a "date to know" box and the office contact details. Open rate sits around 46%.
