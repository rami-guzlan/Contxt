1. Lead with joint papers: lease or deed, the last 3 months of joint bank statements, insurance naming the spouse, a joint tax return.
2. Add no more than 12 photos spread across the relationship, each labeled with date, place and who is in it.
3. Collect two notarized affidavits from people who know the couple, each with a copy of the writer's ID.
4. Tab everything and put a one-page index on top.
5. Teresa reviews the packet before it goes into the filing.
