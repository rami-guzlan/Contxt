On September 2, 2026 Teresa cut the monthly policy news roundup from Puentes. Each issue now carries a single explainer of about 350 words on one question, such as how a work permit renewal works. Explainers were getting roughly three times the clicks, and the roundup cost Teresa two hours of fact-checking a month. The topic is whatever clients asked about most that month.
