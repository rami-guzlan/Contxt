First consultations are 45 minutes and cost $150, charged when the slot is booked. If the person retains the firm within 30 days, the $150 is credited against their flat fee. Free 15-minute screenings happen only at the quarterly citizenship clinic and for people sent by the Newcomer Legal Center. Moving the appointment is free with 24 hours notice; a no-show keeps the fee.
