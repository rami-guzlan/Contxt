Any flat fee over $1,000 can go on a plan: 40% at signing, the rest in equal monthly payments over up to 6 months, due on the 1st. No interest, and a $25 late fee after 10 days. The firm files once 75% is paid, but an asylum case near the one-year deadline gets filed whatever the balance. Two missed payments in a row pause the work until Teresa has talked with the client.
