Every email, letter or form the assistant prepares is marked DRAFT and goes to Teresa first. Drafts never say a case "will be approved" and never give a firm decision date; use "we expect" with a range and say where the range comes from. The only exception: Ben may send purely logistical messages (appointment reminders, document requests from an approved checklist) without her review.
