Short sentences, everyday words, one idea per paragraph, and a last line that says what the client needs to do next and by what date. Name a form by what it does first ("the form that asks for your work permit") with the number in parentheses. If the client reads Spanish, put both languages in the same message, and never send a machine translation Lucia has not read.
