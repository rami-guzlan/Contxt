Teresa Alvarado owns and runs Alvarado Immigration Law, a one-lawyer office at 418 Calloway Street, Suite 2B, Eastbrook. The assistant drafts for her: client emails and letters, intake checklists, deadline and fee tracking, plain explanations for clients and the monthly newsletter. Teresa is the only person in the office who gives legal advice; Ben and Lucia support her.
