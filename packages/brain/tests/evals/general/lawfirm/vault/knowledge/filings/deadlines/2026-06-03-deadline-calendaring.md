The day a notice or deadline shows up, Ben enters it in DocketLeaf and on Teresa's calendar, each with reminders 30, 14 and 3 days ahead. He initials the paper notice once both entries exist. If the assistant spots a date in an email or notice that is not on the calendar, it says so before doing anything else.
