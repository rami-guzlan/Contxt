A client's asylum declaration usually takes three 90-minute sessions with Lucia interpreting. It is written in the client's own words in Spanish first, then Lucia translates it into English and signs the certificate of translation. Plan on about four weeks from first session to signed final, longer if country reports or medical letters are still coming in.
