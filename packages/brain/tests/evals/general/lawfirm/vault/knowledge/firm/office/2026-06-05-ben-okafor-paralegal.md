Ben Okafor has been the firm's full-time paralegal since 2021, in the office 8:30 to 5, Monday to Friday. He fills in forms, checks every fee against the fee sheet, builds and mails packets, and scans each USCIS notice into DocketLeaf (the case management system) the day it arrives. Go to Ben for receipt numbers, tracking numbers and where a file stands; anything that needs legal judgment goes to Teresa.
