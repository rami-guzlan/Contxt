As of June 10, 2026 the firm files online whenever USCIS allows it for that form and case: N-400, I-130 and standalone I-765 renewals. Reasons: $50 less on the N-400 and I-130, a receipt notice the same day, and no packets lost in the mail. Family packets with a concurrent I-485 still go on paper because the bundle cannot be split online.
