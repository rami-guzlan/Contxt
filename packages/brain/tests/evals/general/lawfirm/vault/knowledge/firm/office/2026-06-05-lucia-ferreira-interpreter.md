Lucia Ferreira is the part-time Spanish interpreter and translator, in the office Tuesdays and Thursdays from 9 to 2. Other times she can be booked by text with at least 48 hours notice. She is an independent contractor at $45 an hour and invoices on the last day of the month. She signs the certificate of translation for anything going to USCIS, so every Spanish document translation goes through her.
