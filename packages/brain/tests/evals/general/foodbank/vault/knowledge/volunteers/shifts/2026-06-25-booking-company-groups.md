Groups of 10 or more book through Priya at least three weeks ahead, and no more than 20 from one group so regulars still get spots. Companies are asked for a suggested gift of $300 per session; faith groups and schools are not asked. Everyone signs the online waiver before they arrive. Group sessions are Tuesday and Wednesday mornings only.
