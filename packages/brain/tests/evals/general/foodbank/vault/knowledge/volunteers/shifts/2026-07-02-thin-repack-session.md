48 hours before: if fewer than 12 are signed up, Priya sends one text to the standby list (about 80 regulars who opted in). 24 hours before: still under 10, ask Tomas if two staff can join and post in the regulars group chat. Morning of: under 8, cut the plan to the urgent product only, perishables first. Never cancel a session that has anyone on it without phoning each person.
