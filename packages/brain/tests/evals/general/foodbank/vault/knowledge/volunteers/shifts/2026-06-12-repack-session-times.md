Repack sessions run Tuesday to Saturday, 9:00 to 12:00 and 13:00 to 16:00, plus a Thursday evening slot 17:30 to 20:00. Cap is 25 people per session; the fire limit on the repack floor is 30 counting staff. Ages 14 and 15 come with an adult, one adult for every five teens; 16 and older can come alone. Closed-toe shoes or we send them home.
