Decided 2026-07-15, after three rural agencies kept missing out on chicken and milk: anything flagged "allocated" in the portal (frozen protein, eggs, milk, peanut butter) is split by each agency's share of households served in the previous quarter. Denise runs the split Thursday morning before the portal opens. Whatever is left after 48 hours opens to everyone.
