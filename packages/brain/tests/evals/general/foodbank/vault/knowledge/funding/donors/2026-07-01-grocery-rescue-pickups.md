Store rescue: Oakmere Grocers, three stores (Fairhaven, Pell Crossing, Hadley), picked up by the van Monday, Wednesday and Friday, about 9,000 pounds a month, mostly produce and bakery. Linden Hollow Dairy Co-op drops about 120 cases of milk every Thursday at 6:30, so someone has to be on the dock. Kessler Bakehouse leaves day-old bread on the rack by the side door every evening. Store contacts are in Marcus' donor file.
