Food is counted in pounds, never cases or boxes. Meals equal pounds divided by 1.2. The fiscal year runs July 1 to June 30 and is named for the year it ends, so FY2027 began July 1, 2026. Write dates out in full (October 14, 2026), never as numbers only.
