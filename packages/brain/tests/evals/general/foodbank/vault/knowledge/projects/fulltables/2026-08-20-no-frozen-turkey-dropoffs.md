Decided 2026-08-20: no frozen turkeys from the public this year. Last year 2,300 birds arrived over three days, overflowed the freezer and we lost about 400 pounds. Instead we ask for cash ("$20 buys a turkey") and buy in bulk through our purchasing contract at $1.05 a pound, delivered in two loads. Birds go to agencies by the same household share we use for protein. If someone leaves a turkey at the door anyway, take it, probe it, thank them.
