Purchased product carries a shared maintenance fee of $0.08 a pound; donated food and government commodities carry none. Invoices go out on the 1st and are due in 30 days. An agency with a balance over 60 days can't order purchased items until it's paid. Only Helen can waive fees for an agency in hardship.
