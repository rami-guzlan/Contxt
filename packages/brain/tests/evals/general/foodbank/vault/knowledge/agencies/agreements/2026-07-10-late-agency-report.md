On the 11th the portal sends its automatic reminder and Denise follows with a personal email the same day. On the 15th she phones the agency contact. If a second month in a row is missing, ordering is paused (produce at the Friday walk-up is still allowed) and I get a heads up. Ordering reopens the day the reports arrive.
