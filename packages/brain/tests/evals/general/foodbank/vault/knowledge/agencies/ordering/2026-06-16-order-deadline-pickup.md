The order portal opens Thursday at 8:00 with the new available list and closes Wednesday at 17:00. Orders ship the next week, on the agency's run day or as a dock pickup Monday or Tuesday 8:00 to 13:00. Late orders go to Denise and only get filled from what's left after the runs are packed. Produce and bread are open for walk-up pickup Friday 10:00 to 12:00, no order needed.
