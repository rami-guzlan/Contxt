The walk-in cooler holds 60 pallets at a 34 F set point; the walk-in freezer holds 40 pallets at -5 F. The wireless sensors text Tomas first, me if he hasn't acknowledged within 15 minutes, then Luis. The dock crew writes readings on the clipboard at 7:00 and 15:00 and Tomas scans the sheets to the shared drive every Friday. Service contract is with Fenwick Cold Service, account HH-2210; their 24 hour number is taped on the cooler door.
