Put the ask or the answer in the first line. Short sentences, no jargon like "food insecure individuals" or "leverage"; the people our agencies serve are "neighbors". No exclamation marks, not even in volunteer thank-yous. Keep emails under 150 words unless I ask for more, and sign them "Grace", not the full title.
