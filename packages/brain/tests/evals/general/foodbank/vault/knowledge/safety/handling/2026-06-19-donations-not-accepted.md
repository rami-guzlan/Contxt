We turn away home-canned or homemade food, anything opened or resealed, alcohol, raw milk, and expired infant formula or baby food. Chilled donations get probed at the dock and anything warmer than 41 F is refused on the spot. Dented cans are fine unless the dent is on a seam or the can bulges, leaks or is rusted through. Thank the donor anyway and hand them the "what we can take" card.
