Every member agency re-signs the agreement each July. Main terms: open to the public at least 4 hours a week; no charges, no requested donations and no required worship in exchange for food; no ID, since residence and income are self-declared on the intake sheet; daily fridge and freezer temperature logs; the monthly service report in by the 10th; at least one person at our food safety training every year. Their director and one board member both sign.
