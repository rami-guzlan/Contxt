1. Keep the doors shut; a full freezer holds about 24 hours, the cooler about 4. 2. Call Fenwick Cold Service (account HH-2210) and let Tomas and me know. 3. Write the inside temperature on the outage sheet every 30 minutes. 4. Not fixed within 3 hours: Tomas rents a reefer trailer from the truck lot on Route 9 (about $275 a day) and the crew moves protein first, then dairy, then produce. 5. Afterward apply the 2 hour rule to anything that went above 41 F and record the losses for the insurance claim.
