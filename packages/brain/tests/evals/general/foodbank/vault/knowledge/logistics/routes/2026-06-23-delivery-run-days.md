North run Mondays, 11 stops, off the dock at 7:30. South run Tuesdays, 9 stops. East run Wednesdays, 8 stops including both school pantries. West run through rural Tamsin County every other Thursday, 7 stops and about 140 miles round trip. The other 5 agencies collect at the dock. Every stop gets a 30 minute window, sent to the agency contact the Friday before.
