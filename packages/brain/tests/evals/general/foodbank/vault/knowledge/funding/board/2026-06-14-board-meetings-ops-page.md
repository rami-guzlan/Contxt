The board meets the second Tuesday of January, March, May, July, September and November at 18:00 in the warehouse training room. My operations page is due to Helen one week before. One page, always the same five measures against the same period last year: pounds out, agencies served, volunteer hours, discard rate and on-time delivery rate, then up to three bullets on risks or asks.
