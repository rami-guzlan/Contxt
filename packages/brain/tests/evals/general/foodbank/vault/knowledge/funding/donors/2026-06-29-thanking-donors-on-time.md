Any gift of $250 or more gets a thank-you within 48 hours; Marcus' team sends them and I sign the ones over $1,000 by hand. A food donation over 5,000 pounds gets a phone call from me within the week. In-kind receipts list what came in and its weight only; we never put a dollar value on donated food, that's for the donor to work out for their taxes.
