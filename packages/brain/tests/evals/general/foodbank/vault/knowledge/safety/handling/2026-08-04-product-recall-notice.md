When a recall comes in (state alert email, a supplier call, the news), Tomas searches the inventory system by item and lot code within 2 hours. Matching stock gets red HOLD tags and goes to the hold cage by the back door. Denise pulls every agency that received the lot in the last 60 days, phones each one that day, then sends a written notice. The recall log gets the notice date, lots, pounds on hand, pounds already shipped, and who was told when. Nothing is destroyed or returned until the supplier says how.
