Harvest Hands is a nonprofit founded in 1998. Everything moves through one warehouse at 1180 Mill Creek Road in Fairhaven, which supplies 40 member agencies (church and community pantries, three soup kitchens, two school pantries) across Wrenfield, Oxley and Tamsin counties. 12 staff and roughly 650 volunteers active in the last 12 months. FY2026 closed June 30 at 3.1 million pounds out, about 2.6 million meals.
