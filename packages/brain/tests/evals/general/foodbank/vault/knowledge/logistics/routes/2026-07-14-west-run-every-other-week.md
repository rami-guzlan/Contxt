Decided 2026-07-14: the West run goes from weekly to every other Thursday starting August 6, with double-size drops. All seven West agencies have the freezer room for it (checked on site visits). Saves about $1,100 a month in fuel and driver overtime. We review it at the end of December; if an agency runs short of protein in the off week, Luis adds a van drop.
