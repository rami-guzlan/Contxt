Barrels go out to sites by October 30. Volunteer drivers in their own cars empty each barrel weekly, or sooner when the site calls to say it's three-quarters full; they weigh it on the dock scale and log the site name. Turkey loads arrive November 9 and 16 and agencies have theirs by November 20 (Thanksgiving is November 26). Last barrel pickup is January 4, 2027, and totals close January 8.
