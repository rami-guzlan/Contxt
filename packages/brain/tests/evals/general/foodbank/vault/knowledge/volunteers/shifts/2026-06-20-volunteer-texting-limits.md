Texts to volunteers go out only between 8:00 and 20:00. The whole list gets at most two blasts a week; a text to one session's sign-ups doesn't count toward that. First names, under 300 characters, and end with "Reply STOP to opt out". Anything longer goes in the email newsletter, which goes out the first Monday of the month.
