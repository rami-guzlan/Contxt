Agency monthly reports reach us as counts only, and that's how they stay. Nothing that identifies a person we serve (name, address, phone, photo, who lives in the household) goes into a funder report, board packet, newsletter or social post unless they signed our story release, which Marcus keeps on file. If an agency emails a sign-in sheet with names, delete the attachment and ask them for totals instead.
