Larvae up to 5 days post fertilisation are not protected animals, so anyone trained in the lab may work with them. From day 6 on, and for all adults, only people holding a personal licence and listed on AEL-2025-117 may handle, cross or fin clip. At the moment that means Ines, Arjun and Hanna. Anyone new who needs fish work first takes the personal licence course; the next one starts 2026-11-09.
