Add a line to the shared Orders sheet with catalogue number, supplier, quantity, price, grant code and who it is for. Marco Bellini puts orders through the university procurement portal every Monday and Thursday morning. Anything above 1,000 EUR needs Elena's tick in the sheet first, and above 5,000 EUR procurement wants three quotes. For something urgent message Marco directly, but the sheet line is still needed.
