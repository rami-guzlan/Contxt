First check the door is shut and read the display; a door left ajar usually recovers within 20 minutes. If it reads above -65 °C and is still climbing, move racks into backup Freezer 3 in B210 (its top two shelves are kept empty for exactly this), carrying them on dry ice from the cold room. Then ring down the call list taped to the freezer door, Marco first, Elena second. Finish by logging the time, the temperature and which racks moved in the freezer log in the ELN.
