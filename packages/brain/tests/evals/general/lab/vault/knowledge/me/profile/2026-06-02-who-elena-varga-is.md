Elena Varga is an associate professor in the Department of Molecular Biosciences at Northmere University and runs the Varga Lab: three postdocs, five PhD students and one technician. She is the PI on every lab grant and personally signs off spending, safety registrations and manuscripts. Her office is B205; the main lab is B210 and the tissue culture room is B214, all on the second floor of the Harrow Building.
