The lab's BSL-2 registration, BR-2024-063, allows third-generation lentiviral vectors in B214 and nowhere else, so making virus or transducing cells in the main lab B210 is out. Only people on the BSL-2 register kept by Tomasz Kowal may do this work. Liquid waste gets bleach made up that day, 10% final, with 30 minutes contact before it goes down the sink, and all solid waste goes into the orange autoclave bags in B214.
