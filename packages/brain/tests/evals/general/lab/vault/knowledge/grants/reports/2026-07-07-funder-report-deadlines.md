The Kestrel Foundation annual progress report for KF-2024-031 is due 2026-11-15, submitted through their applicant portal. The Regional Research Council final report for RRC-22-4410 is due 2027-03-31, with a separate financial statement that Freya Lund prepares. Research finance needs the financial part of any report two weeks before the funder's date.
