The lab works on stress granules, the RNA and protein condensates neurons form under stress, and why they fail to dissolve in models of neurodegeneration. Two systems: zebrafish larvae (spinal motor neurons, imaged live) and SH-SY5Y neuroblastoma cells carrying a tagged G3BP1. Core methods are confocal live imaging, CLIP and RNA-seq, CRISPR knockouts in fish and lentiviral reporter lines in cells.
