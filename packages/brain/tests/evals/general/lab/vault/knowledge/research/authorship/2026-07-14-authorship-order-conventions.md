Whoever did most of the experiments and wrote the first draft is first author; Elena is last and corresponding author on every lab paper. The list and order are agreed at a meeting before anyone starts writing, and recorded in the paper's authorship log. Marco is a co-author when he generated data shown in the paper, not for ordering or upkeep alone. Any later change needs everyone on the list to agree.
