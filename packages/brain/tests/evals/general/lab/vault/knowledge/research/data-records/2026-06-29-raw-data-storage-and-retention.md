Microscope images, sequencing reads and gel scans go onto the VargaLab-Raw share on university research storage within one week of acquisition, and never sit only on a laptop or USB stick. The share is backed up nightly and folders turn read-only after 30 days. University policy and the Kestrel data management plan both require keeping raw data for 10 years after the last paper that uses it. Leavers hand their folders over to Jonas before their account closes.
