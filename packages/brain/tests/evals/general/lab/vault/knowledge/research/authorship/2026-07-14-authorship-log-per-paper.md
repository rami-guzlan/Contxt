Each manuscript folder on LabDrive has an authorship_log.xlsx listing everyone considered, their contribution roles (conceptualisation, investigation, formal analysis and so on) and the date the order was agreed. Journals ask for a contributions statement at submission, and it gets written from this file. Elena checks it before anything is submitted.
