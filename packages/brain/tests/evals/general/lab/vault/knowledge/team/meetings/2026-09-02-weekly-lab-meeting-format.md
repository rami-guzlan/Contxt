Lab meeting is Wednesday 10:00 to 11:30 in seminar room C301. One person gives a 30-minute data talk, everyone else gives a two-minute update, and the last 15 minutes cover admin such as orders, safety and bookings. The presenter drops slides into LabDrive > Meetings by Tuesday 17:00. On the last Wednesday of the month journal club takes the place of the data talk.
