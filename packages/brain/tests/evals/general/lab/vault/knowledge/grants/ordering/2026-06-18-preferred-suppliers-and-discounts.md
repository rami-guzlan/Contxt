General reagents and kits come from Bellwether Scientific under the university framework contract: 12% off list and free next-day delivery over 150 EUR. Antibodies mostly from Corvane Biotech, whose rep sends sample sizes if asked before buying a full vial. Tips, tubes and plates come from Fenwick Plastics, bulk-ordered once a quarter by Marco. Going off-framework for anything above 500 EUR triggers an extra procurement check, so avoid it.
