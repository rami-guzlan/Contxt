The lab notebook is the LabLedger ELN; each experiment gets an entry on the day it is done, and entries are signed off every Friday. Files follow YYYYMMDD_initials_experiment_rep, for example 20261012_LB_G3BP1-arsenite_r2.tif. The ELN entry links to the raw data folder instead of holding big files itself.
