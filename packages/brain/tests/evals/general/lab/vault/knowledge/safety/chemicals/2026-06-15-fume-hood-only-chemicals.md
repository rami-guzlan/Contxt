Paraformaldehyde (powder, and any solution above 1%), phenol, chloroform, beta-mercaptoethanol and acrylamide powder are opened and handled only in one of the two fume hoods in B210, with nitrile gloves and goggles. Fixing fish in PFA happens in the hood too. The ready-made 4% PFA aliquots are kept in the small fridge next to the hood, never in the main fridge.
