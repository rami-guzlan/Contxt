Before the start date, ask department administrator Beatrix Moll for the key card and a desk. In week one the newcomer collects the card (B214 access is added only after BSL-2 training), completes the EHS online chemical safety and biosafety courses, gets LabDrive and ELN accounts from Jonas and has a lab tour with Marco. Assign a postdoc buddy, and the newcomer reads and signs lab handbook v4 (2026) before any bench work. Book a 30-minute check-in with Elena at the end of week two.
