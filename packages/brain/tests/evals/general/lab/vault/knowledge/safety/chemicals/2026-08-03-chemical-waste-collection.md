Write full chemical names and rough percentages on the yellow waste label; abbreviations like PFA or Tris get the container refused. Halogenated and non-halogenated solvents go in separate containers, and the B210 satellite area never holds more than 20 L in total. Send the EHS waste pickup form by Monday 12:00 and the collection comes Wednesday morning. Marco usually files it, but anyone may.
