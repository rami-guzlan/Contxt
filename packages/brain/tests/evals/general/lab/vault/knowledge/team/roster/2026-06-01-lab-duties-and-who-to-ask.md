Ordering, stock levels, chemical waste and instrument service contracts sit with Marco. Samuel is the lab safety representative and first aider. Jonas looks after the analysis server, storage quotas and the ELN admin account, and Ines handles fish licence paperwork and liaison with the facility. Students go to their postdoc mentor first for day-to-day questions, and to Elena for funding, authorship or thesis committee matters.
