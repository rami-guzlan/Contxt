The imaging core on the ground floor has the two confocals the lab uses: the spinning disk for live fish and the point scanner for fixed cells. Slots are reserved in FacilityHub against the KF-2024-031 account; the internal rate is 38 EUR per hour, dropping to 19 EUR after 18:00 and at weekends. Daytime use is capped at 4 hours per person per day. FacilityHub only unlocks a microscope after a 2-hour induction with core manager Priya Nandakumar.
