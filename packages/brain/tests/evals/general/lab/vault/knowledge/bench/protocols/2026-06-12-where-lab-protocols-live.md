Approved methods live in LabDrive > Protocols as PDFs numbered VL-P-001 upwards, with the version in the file name, for example VL-P-014_v3_Western-blot.pdf. The editable sources sit in Protocols/Drafts and do not count as approved until Elena signs the PDF. Safety SOPs are their own series, SOP-VL-01 to SOP-VL-12, in the Safety folder. Marco keeps the index spreadsheet current.
