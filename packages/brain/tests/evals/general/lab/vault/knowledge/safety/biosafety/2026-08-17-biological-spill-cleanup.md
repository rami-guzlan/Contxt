Spill inside the cabinet: lay paper towels over it, pour fresh 10% bleach from the edges inwards, leave 30 minutes, wipe up and autoclave the lot. Spill outside the cabinet (virus or human cells): warn people, leave the room for 30 minutes so aerosols settle, then go back in with gown, gloves and eye protection and do the same. Any spill outside the cabinet goes on the EHS incident form that same day, and Elena is told.
