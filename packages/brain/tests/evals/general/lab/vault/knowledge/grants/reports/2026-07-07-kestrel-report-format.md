Kestrel caps progress reports at four pages, plus a separate outputs list of papers, preprints, datasets and talks. The lay summary is 250 words and goes up on their website, so it must not mention anything unpublished. Elena likes to open with one figure and the three main findings, then risks and any changes to the plan.
