At the 2026-06-22 lab meeting Elena moved routine RNA extraction from cultured cells off acid phenol and onto the silica column kit, written up as VL-P-021 v1, mainly to cut phenol use and solvent waste. Phenol extraction stays only for pooled zebrafish embryos, where the columns gave too little RNA. The old cell protocol VL-P-009 is retired and moved to the archive folder.
