British spelling throughout (licence, colour, analyse). SI units with a space before the unit, like 5 mL, 37 °C and 300 x g, and concentrations in mM or µg/mL rather than vague percentages. Times on the 24-hour clock and dates written 2026-10-14. Three or four sentences per paragraph at most; she cuts anything longer.
