1) Start from Luis's walk-through notes, photos and measurements. 2) Break the work out room by room, keeping labor, materials and subs as separate lines internally. 3) Add allowance lines for any finishes the homeowner has not chosen yet. 4) For kitchens and baths in houses built before 1960, add a 10% contingency for surprises behind the walls. 5) Save it as "<last name> <job type> v1" and send it to Luis; it reaches the homeowner only after he approves it, and it is good for 30 days from the date sent.
