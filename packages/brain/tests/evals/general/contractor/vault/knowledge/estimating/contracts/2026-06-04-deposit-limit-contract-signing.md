State home improvement rules and our own contract cap the up-front deposit at 25% of the contract price, special-order cabinets included. Do not take more, even when a homeowner offers to pay everything early. No cash: deposits come by check or bank transfer only, and Amy issues a receipt. Breaking this can void the contract and put our registration at risk.
