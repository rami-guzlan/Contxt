Amy requests the inspection on the Wrenfield building department portal; anything in by 3:00 pm gets the next business day. The town gives a morning (8 to 12) or afternoon (12 to 4) window, and the crew lead stays on site the whole window with the permit card and the stamped drawings. After a pass, photograph the signed card and drop it in the job folder. A failed inspection costs a $75 reinspection fee; rebook it the same day.
