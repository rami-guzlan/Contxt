From 6:30 to about 10:00 Luis is out walking job sites and does not pick up calls. Afternoons from 1:00 he is in the office, and estimate writing happens Tuesday and Thursday afternoons. Sundays the phone is off unless it is a water leak or something structural. He likes a text for a quick yes or no and an email for anything with prices in it.
