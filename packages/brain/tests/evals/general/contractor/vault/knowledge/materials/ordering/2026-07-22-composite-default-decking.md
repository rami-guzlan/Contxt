Decided July 22, 2026: new deck estimates default to the yard's capped composite boards (grey or brown) on pressure-treated framing. Pressure-treated boards for the walking surface are now quoted only when a homeowner asks. Reason: four callbacks in 2025 for cupping and splinters on wood decks. Composite adds about $11 per square foot.
