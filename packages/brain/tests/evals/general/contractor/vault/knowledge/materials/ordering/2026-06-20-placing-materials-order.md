1) Build the takeoff from the approved estimate, never an earlier draft. 2) Have the crew lead check the quantities. 3) Use the job number as the PO number, and route anything over $2,500 through Amy. 4) Ask for delivery by 7:00 am on the working day before the start date, with the drop spot marked on the site map. 5) The crew lead checks the delivery ticket against the order and reports any shorts to Amy the same day.
