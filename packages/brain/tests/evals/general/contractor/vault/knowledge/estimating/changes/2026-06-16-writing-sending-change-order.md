Number it CO-<job number>-<sequence>, for example CO-2614-03. Describe the change in one or two plain sentences, then give the price (labor plus materials at the usual markup) and how many days it adds. Luis approves the draft, Amy sends it for e-signature, and the crew lead gets a copy once it comes back signed. Change orders are paid in full with the next scheduled draw.
