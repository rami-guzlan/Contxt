1) Amy logs the call in the callback sheet with the job number and asks the homeowner for photos. 2) Luis reviews the photos and decides within two business days whether it is warranty, a paid repair or not our work. 3) Warranty repairs go into a Friday buffer slot, with the original crew when possible. 4) After the visit, send the homeowner a short note saying what was fixed.
