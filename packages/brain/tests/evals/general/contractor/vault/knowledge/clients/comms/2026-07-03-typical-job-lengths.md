Time on site once materials are here: kitchen 5 to 7 weeks, full bath 3 to 4 weeks, half bath about 8 working days, deck 1 to 2 weeks depending on size and stairs. Add permit review (2 to 3 weeks) and, for kitchens, the cabinet lead time. Families usually stay in the house; during a kitchen job we set up a temporary sink and microwave station in the dining room.
