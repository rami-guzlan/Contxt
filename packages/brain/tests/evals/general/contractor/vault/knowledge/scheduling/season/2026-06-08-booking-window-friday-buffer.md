title: Booking window and Friday buffer

Do not put a start date on the calendar more than 10 weeks ahead; past that, give the homeowner a month and confirm the exact date later. Fridays stay open for punch lists, callbacks and weather catch-up, so no job ever starts on a Friday. A start date is firm only after the deposit has cleared.
