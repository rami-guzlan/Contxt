Keep it short: greeting, the point, the next step with a date. No trade jargon (say "the inspector checks the wiring before we close the walls", not "rough-in"). Bad news such as a delay or an added cost goes in the first sentence, never buried. Sign as "Luis Ferreira, Ferreira Renovations", and never promise a finish date before the cabinets and the permit are in hand. Every message gets an answer within one business day (water leaks and safety problems within the hour), and each active job gets a Friday update by 3:00 pm saying what got done, what is next and anything we need from them.
