Tile gets 10% extra for a straight lay and 15% for diagonal or herringbone. Decking boards get 8%, framing lumber 5%, and drywall is rounded up per wall plus 2 spare sheets. Unopened tile boxes left at the end stay with the homeowner for future repairs, labeled by room.
