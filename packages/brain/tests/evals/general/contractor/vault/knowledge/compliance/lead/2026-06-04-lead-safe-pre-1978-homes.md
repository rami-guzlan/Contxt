Before any demolition, sanding or cutting of painted surfaces in a house built before 1978, we test the paint or treat it as lead. A certified renovator then sets up plastic containment, with no dry sanding or torch stripping, HEPA vacuum cleanup and a cleaning check at the end. The homeowner signs the receipt for the lead-hazard pamphlet before work starts, and Amy keeps it in the job file for three years. Skipping steps risks large fines and, worse, a child's health.
