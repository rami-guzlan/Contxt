Materials carry an 18% markup, subcontractor invoices 12%, and equipment rentals and dumpsters 10%. Target gross margin is 32% on a job; if a draft lands under 28%, flag it to Luis before anything else happens. Markup never shows as its own line for the homeowner: it is folded into each line item.
