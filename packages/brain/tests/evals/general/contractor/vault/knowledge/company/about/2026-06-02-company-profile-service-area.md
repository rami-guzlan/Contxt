Ferreira Renovations does residential kitchens, bathrooms and decks, plus the carpentry those jobs drag along (trim, small framing repairs, porch steps). Registered home improvement contractor HIC-55190, with the shop and office at 14 Tannery Road, Wrenfield. We work within roughly 30 minutes' drive of the shop; farther jobs carry a $150 per week travel charge or get turned down. No roofing, additions, full basement finishing or commercial work. Around 45 jobs and $1.9M in revenue in 2025.
