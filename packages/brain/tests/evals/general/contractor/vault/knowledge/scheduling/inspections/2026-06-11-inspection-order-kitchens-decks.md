Kitchens and baths: rough electrical and rough plumbing (the subs book those), then framing, then insulation if walls were opened, then final. Decks: footing holes before any concrete, framing before the boards go down, then final. No drywall or tile backer goes up until every rough inspection has passed. Gail Pruitt inspects most of our area and always checks that joist hangers are fully nailed.
