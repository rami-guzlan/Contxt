Electrical goes to Kestrel Electric (owner Joe Raddick), who needs two weeks' notice for rough-in and pulls his own electrical permit. Plumbing goes to Halvorsen Plumbing (Nils Halvorsen), one week's notice, also pulls his own permit, and bills a $140 trip charge if the site is not ready when he shows up. When Kestrel is full, Arlo Vance takes small electrical jobs only. Both firms invoice us directly, and we add our sub markup.
