Decided July 8, 2026: every estimate lists tile, plumbing fixtures, lighting and hardware as allowance lines with a stated amount (tile material at $9 per square foot is the usual figure). Three spring kitchens ran over because homeowners upgraded tile, and it looked like our overrun. Anything above an allowance is billed by change order at cost plus 18%.
