Nothing outside the signed scope gets started until the homeowner has signed a change order showing the price and the extra days. A verbal OK on site does not count, not even from a repeat client. For urgent finds (hidden rot, a leaking supply line), Luis can approve a stop-gap fix up to $500 and the paperwork follows the same day.
