In Wrenfield a permit is needed for any deck attached to the house or more than 30 inches off the ground, any new or relocated wiring or plumbing, removing or moving walls, and widening window or door openings. No permit for swapping cabinets and counters in place, flooring, painting, or changing fixtures on existing lines. If it is unclear, Amy calls the permit desk before we quote.
