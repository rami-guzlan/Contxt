Workmanship is covered for two years from the final walkthrough date. Manufacturer warranties on cabinets, fixtures and decking pass straight through to the homeowner. Not covered: hairline grout or caulk cracks from normal settling after the first year, damage from misuse, and anything the homeowner bought and supplied themselves.
