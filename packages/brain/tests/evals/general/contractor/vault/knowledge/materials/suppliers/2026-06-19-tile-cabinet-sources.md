Tile and stone come from Corvane Tile and Stone (10% trade discount), and homeowners pick in the showroom with Bea Lindgren. Cabinets come from Oakhollow Cabinetry: stock lines in about 2 weeks, semi-custom 6 to 7 weeks after the drawings are signed off, so no kitchen start date gets set until Oakhollow confirms a ship date. Counters are templated by Granmere Fabrication once the cabinets are in, then installed about 10 working days later.
