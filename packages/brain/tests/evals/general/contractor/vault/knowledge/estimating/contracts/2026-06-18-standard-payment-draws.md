Kitchens and baths: 25% at signing, 35% when the rough inspections pass, 30% once cabinets and tile are set, 10% after the punch-list walkthrough. Decks: 25% at signing, 50% when framing passes, 25% at final. Invoices are due in 7 days, and Renata Osei, the bookkeeper (in on Tuesdays and Thursdays), sends the reminders.
