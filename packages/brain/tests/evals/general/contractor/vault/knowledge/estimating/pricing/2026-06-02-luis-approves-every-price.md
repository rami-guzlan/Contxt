Anything with a dollar figure in it (estimates, change orders, allowance overages, even a quick "roughly what would that cost" answer) is a draft until Luis has seen it. Send drafts to him by email, not text, and say what changed since the last version. If a homeowner pushes for a number before he has looked, the answer is "Luis will have that to you by" plus a date, never a guess. Amy can resend an approved document but cannot change its price.
