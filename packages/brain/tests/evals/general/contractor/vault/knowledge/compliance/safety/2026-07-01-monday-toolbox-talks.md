Every Monday at 7:00 each crew lead runs a 10-minute safety talk and logs the topic and who attended in the red safety binder kept in the truck. On decks, anyone working more than 6 feet up is tied off or working behind a guard. Ladders extend 3 feet past the landing, no exceptions.
