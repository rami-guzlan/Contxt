# bistro

Marco Bellini is the chef and owner of Bellini's, a 48-seat neighborhood bistro open Tuesday to Sunday dinner plus Sunday brunch. He works with a sous chef (Hana), a front-of-house manager (Devon) and a small line team. He uses his AI assistant for menus and specials, supplier orders, food cost, staff schedules, allergy handling, private events, the restaurant's newsletter and Instagram, and a planned patio opening in spring.
