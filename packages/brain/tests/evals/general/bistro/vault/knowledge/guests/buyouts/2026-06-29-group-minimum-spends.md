The back room seats up to 12 with a food and drink minimum of $900 Tuesday to Thursday and $1,400 on Friday or Saturday; it is not offered for Sunday brunch. Taking over the whole restaurant is only possible on a Monday, when it is normally closed, with a $3,200 minimum for up to 48 seated or 70 standing. Minimums are before tax and the 20% service charge.
