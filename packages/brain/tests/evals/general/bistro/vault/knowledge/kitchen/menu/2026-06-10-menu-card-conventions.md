title: Conventions for the printed menu card
summary: Dish name then its main parts in lowercase, allergen letters after the price, never more than six starters, six mains and four desserts.

Each line is a short dish name followed by its main parts in lowercase, comma separated, for example "Short rib, polenta, gremolata, pickled shallot". Allergen letters follow the price: G gluten, D dairy, N tree nuts, P peanut, S shellfish, F fish, E egg. The card holds at most six starters, six mains and four desserts. Vegetarian dishes get a small v, never a leaf icon.
