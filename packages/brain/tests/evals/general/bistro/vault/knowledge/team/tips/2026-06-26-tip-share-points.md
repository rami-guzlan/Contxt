Every hourly worker earns points per hour on shift: servers and bar 1.0, bussers 0.6, line cooks and dish 0.5. The pool for the pay period is divided by the total points, then multiplied out per person. Shares go out with payroll every second Friday. Devon keeps the tip sheet and Marco signs it.
