1) Tuesday morning Hana walks the walk-in and pitches one starter, one main and one dessert by 2 pm, built on whatever is overstocked. 2) Each one is costed and must come in under 27% plate cost. 3) Marco tastes at 4 pm. 4) Servers get a two-minute briefing at 5.10 and the chalkboard is done before doors at 5.30. They run until they sell out or the next Tuesday.
