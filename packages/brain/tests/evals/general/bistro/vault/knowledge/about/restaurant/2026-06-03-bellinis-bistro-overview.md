Bellini's opened in March 2019 on Corliss Street. Seating is 28 in the main room (tables 1 to 12), 12 in the back room (tables 20 to 23, kept for groups) and 8 stools at the bar. The kitchen is open to the room: six-burner range, plancha, pasta station and a single fryer everything shares. The average check runs about $62 a head with drinks.
