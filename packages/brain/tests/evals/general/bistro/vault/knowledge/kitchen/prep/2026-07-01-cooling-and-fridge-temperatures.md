Stocks, braises and sauces must cool from 57C to 21C inside two hours, then to 5C inside the next four, in ice baths and shallow hotel pans. The walk-in has to read 3C or colder and the reach-ins 4C or colder. Readings go on the clipboard by the walk-in door at 3 pm and at close, with initials. Anything that misses the window goes in the bin.
