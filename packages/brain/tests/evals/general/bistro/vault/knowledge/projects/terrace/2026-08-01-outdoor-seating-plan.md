The plan is 16 seats outside (eight two-tops) along the side lane, reached through the back room door. Dinner only, 5.30 to 9.30 pm, with no brunch outdoors in the first season. Target opening is Tuesday 2027-04-13, weather permitting. It needs a city sidewalk seating permit, a ramp over the step and two extra servers on busy nights.
