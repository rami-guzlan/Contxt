1) The closing cook does the stock count on the inventory sheet Sunday night. 2) Monday morning, compare the counts with the pars in the shared order sheet and fill in the order column for each supplier. 3) Check the week's bookings and any buyouts and adjust up. 4) Marco reviews the drafts by noon. 5) Send each order before its cutoff and paste the confirmation into the sheet. Short or damaged items are photographed at the door and claimed for credit the same day.
