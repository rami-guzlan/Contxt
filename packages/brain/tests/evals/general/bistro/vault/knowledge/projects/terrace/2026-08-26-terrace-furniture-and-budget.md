On 2026-08-26 Marco capped the outdoor seating project at $18,500. That covers eight folding teak tables and sixteen chairs from Hedgeway Outdoor ($6,200), planters and a rope barrier ($2,100), two heaters ($3,400), the ramp ($2,800) and the permit and drawings ($1,600), leaving $2,400 for surprises. He picked folding pieces so they can live in the back room from November to March.
