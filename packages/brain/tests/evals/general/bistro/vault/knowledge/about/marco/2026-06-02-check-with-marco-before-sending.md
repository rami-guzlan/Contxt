Anything that leaves the building is a draft until Marco has seen it: orders to suppliers, replies to guests, the monthly email, captions, rota changes sent to staff. If he is mid-service, leave the draft with a one-line note saying what it is and when it has to go. He started this after a double fish order went out unchecked in May 2026.
