1) Write the recipe card with gram weights per portion. 2) Price every ingredient from the latest invoice, never from memory. 3) Add 5% for trim and waste plus 30 cents for oil and garnish. 4) Divide that plate cost by 0.29 (or 0.27 for a special) to get the selling price. 5) Round to a whole number and check it sits sensibly next to the dishes around it on the card.
