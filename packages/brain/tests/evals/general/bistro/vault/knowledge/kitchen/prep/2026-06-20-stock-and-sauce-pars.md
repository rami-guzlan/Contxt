The morning prep cook works to these pars: veal stock 20 liters on Tuesday and Friday, chicken stock 15 liters every day, ragu base 12 liters on Tuesday and Saturday, tomato sauce 8 liters daily. Pasta dough is 6 kg on weeknights and 9 kg on Friday and Saturday. If anything drops below half par during service, tell Hana, not Marco.
