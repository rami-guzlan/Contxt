The email reaches about 2,300 people, with opens around 48%, and goes out on the first Thursday of the month at 10 am. It always has three parts: what changed on the card, one date worth booking, and a short kitchen tip from Marco. One photo only, taken in the restaurant. The draft is due to Marco the Monday before.
