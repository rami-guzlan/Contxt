Since 2026-07-21 cream, butter, cheese and eggs come from Haldane Creamery instead of Copperbrook Dairy. Copperbrook was late four Fridays running in June and July, and twice brunch ran short on cream. Haldane charges $4.10 a pound for butter against $4.50 and delivers Tuesday and Friday before 7 am. Do not reorder from Copperbrook without asking Marco.
