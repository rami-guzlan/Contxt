Marco does not do discounts: no promo codes, no "20% off", no free bottle offers, in the email or anywhere else. The one perk is a free dessert for subscribers in their birthday month, which servers honor if the guest mentions it. If a draft needs a hook, lead with a new dish or a date to book instead.
