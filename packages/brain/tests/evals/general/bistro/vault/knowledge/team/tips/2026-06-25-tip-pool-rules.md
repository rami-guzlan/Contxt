Card and cash gratuities go into one pool shared by hourly staff only: servers, bar, bussers, line cooks and dish. Managers and owners (Marco, Devon, Hana) never take a cut; that is the law, not a house preference. Pool money is never used to cover broken glass, comped plates, card processing fees or a short till. If a payroll note or policy draft would touch the pool, stop and check with Marco.
