Marco trained in Parma and spent ten years in hotel kitchens before opening Bellini's in 2019. His food is Northern Italian at heart without being strict about it: pasta rolled every day, slow braises, lots of vegetables, very little on the plate. He would rather pull a dish than serve a tired ingredient.
