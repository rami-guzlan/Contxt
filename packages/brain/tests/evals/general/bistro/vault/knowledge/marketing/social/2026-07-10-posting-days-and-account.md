The account posts three times a week, Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday at 4 pm, when people are deciding where to eat. The specials board goes up as a story every Tuesday once it is chalked. In July 2026 it had about 4,100 followers, most of them within two miles. Devon has the login; Marco approves every caption.
