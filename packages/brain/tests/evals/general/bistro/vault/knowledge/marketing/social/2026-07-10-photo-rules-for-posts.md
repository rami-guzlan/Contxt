Plates are photographed at the pass or by the front window in daylight, never with filters and never with stock images. A guest shows up in a post only if they said yes on the spot, and staff only if they are happy to be in it. Tag the farm or supplier whenever their produce is on the plate. Captions are two lines at most, in Marco's voice.
