Marco writes the way he talks: short sentences, first person, never "exquisite", "decadent", "elevated" or "curated". Prices on the card are whole numbers with no dollar sign (26, not $26.00). Anything for staff is a bulleted list they can read in thirty seconds. One exclamation mark per message at most, and only for good news.
