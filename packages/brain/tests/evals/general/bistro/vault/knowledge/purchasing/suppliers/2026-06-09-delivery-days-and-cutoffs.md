Tollgate produce arrives Tuesday to Saturday around 8 am if the text goes in by 9 pm the night before. Gullrock fish comes Tuesday and Friday, ordered by noon the day before. Ostrander meat drops Wednesday and Saturday, with one Monday 6 pm cutoff covering both. Bramblegate dry goods come only on Thursday and must be ordered by Tuesday night.
