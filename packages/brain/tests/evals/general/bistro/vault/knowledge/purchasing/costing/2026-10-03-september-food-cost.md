As of 2026-10-03, September 2026 closed at 31.2% food cost against the 29% target (August was 29.8%). Most of the jump is short rib, up 14% at Ostrander since August, plus two private brunches priced before the rise. Marco is weighing a 2 dollar increase on the short rib at the December menu change. Until then, no new beef specials.
