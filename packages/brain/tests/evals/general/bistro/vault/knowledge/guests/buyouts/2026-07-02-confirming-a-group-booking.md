1) Devon replies to every inquiry within 24 hours with open dates and the minimums. 2) The host picks one of three set menus at 65, 85 or 110 a head, and Hana adjusts for any allergies. 3) A 30% deposit holds the date, refundable up to 14 days before. 4) Final numbers and dietary needs are due 5 days out. 5) The balance and the 20% service charge are settled on the night.
